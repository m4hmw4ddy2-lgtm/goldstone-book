TRANSCRIPTION

Yesterday William Gouldstone, the Walthamstow murderer, was removed from Chelmsford Prison to Broadmoor Criminal
Lunatic Asylum, in accordance with the order of the Home Secretary.

TRANSCRIBER'S NOTES

1. "Yesterday" in a paper of Tuesday 16 October: Monday 15 October 1883. Agrees with the Broadmoor admission date and
   with Graves's letter of 11 October / Guardian of 20 October ("left H.M.'s Prison, Chelmsford, on Monday morning").
   A second, independent source for the date of removal.
