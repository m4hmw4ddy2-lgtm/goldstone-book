Municipal elections in Rochester, Chatham and Gillingham will take place on Wednesday, Nov. 1st, and considerable
progress has already been made in the selection of candidates to contest the various wards. The following is a
statement of the position in regard to the elections so far as it is known at present:—

There will be contests in several Chatham wards. The Conservative candidates have already been chosen: Clr. Percy
Ansell in St. John's ward, Clr. F. H. Lawrence in St. Michael's, Clr. W. Keary in St. Mary's, Clr. G. B. Silver in
Luton, and Clr. M. Packer in Christ Church, all of whom have to retire through the expiration of their term of office,
and will seek to retain their seats.

There is a vacancy in St. Paul's ward which has not been filled since the death of Clr. W. G. Snelling, and here Mr
E. T. Bradley, who has fought unsuccessfully in the past, will be the nominee of the Conservative party. He will be
opposed by Mr W. Blackmore, who has previously held a seat on the Town Council.

The only Labour candidate at present selected is Mr A. Goldstone, the propaganda secretary, who will oppose Clr.
Packer at Christ Church. The choice of other candidates by the party is still under consideration.
