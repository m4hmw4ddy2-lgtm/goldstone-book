TRANSCRIPTION

The Late Mr. C. E. Housden.—The funeral took place on Tuesday week of Mr. Cecil Edgar Housden, who died at his home,
6, West Field, West Road. Mr. Housden, who was 64 years of age, was the second son of the late Mr. and Mrs. Frank
Housden, of Peaslands Road, Saffron Walden. He was, for many years, a well-known farmer. The funeral service, at High
Street Baptist Church, was conducted by the Rev. G. W. Sharman, with Mr. Anthony at the organ. During the service the
hymn "How Sweet the Name of Jesus Sounds" was sung, and "O Rest in the Lord" was played as the cortege left the
church for the interment, which followed at the Borough Cemetery. Family mourners were: Mrs. C. Housden (widow), Mr.
