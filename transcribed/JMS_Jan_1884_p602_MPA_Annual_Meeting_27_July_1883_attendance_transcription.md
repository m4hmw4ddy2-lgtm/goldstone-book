## Notes for the chapter (Claude, 27 October 2026; not part of the transcription)
1. **G. H. SAVAGE WAS PRESENT** (the list covers the meeting, not a session; whether he was in the room for the
   afternoon vote is not shown). The one specialist heard at William's trial seven weeks later.
2. **LIONEL A. WEATHERLY WAS PRESENT**: the man who opened the Bristol discussion of 1894. Also present: Bucknill,
   D. Hack Tuke, Yellowlees, D. Bower, Orange (presiding), and "Dr. Nugent, of Dublin".
3. **"A. C. Clark"** is in the list. William was examined at Chelmsford by Orange and "Dr Clarke, from London" (Sunday
   Dispatch, 30 Sept 1883; Times, 2 Oct), whose identity is NOT established (dramatis_personae.md). A LEAD ONLY: the
   spelling differs, and nothing here links them. Not for prose.
4. The day: "Friday, 27th July" (agrees with the address's heading).

## TRANSCRIPTION

PART IV.—NOTES AND NEWS.

THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF THE MEDICO-PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSOCIATION, 1883.

The annual meeting of the Medico-Psychological Association was held on Friday, 27th July, at the Royal College of
Physicians, London, Dr. Orange presiding. The following members and visitors were present:—Drs. C. Aldridge,
Alliott, D. Bower, W. Burman, R. Boyd, Blandford, Fletcher Beach, Bucknill, J. Crichton Browne, E. Maziere Courtenay,
D. Cassidy, P. E. Campbell, J. A. Campbell, A. C. Clark, F. P. Davies, English, J. T. Hingston, W. W. Ireland,
O. Jepson, J. Murray Lindsay, Thos. Lyle, H. J. Manning, Donald Mackintosh, G. Mickley, W. J. Mickle, John Manley,
M. D. Macleod, G. W. Mould, H. H. Newington, A. Newington, D. M. M'Cullough, T. W. McDowall, W. Orange, G. H. Pedler,
H. T. Pringle, H. Rayner, A. H. Stocker, H. Sutherland, G. H. Savage, Arthur Strange, Edward Swain, J. Beveridge
Spence, George Thompson, E. Toller, D. Hack Tuke, C. Molesworth Tuke, John A. Wallis, W. E. R. Wood, A. Law Wade,
Francis J. Wright, J. F. Wright, Lionel A. Weatherly, E. S. Willett, H. Winslow, T. Outterson Wood, D. Yellowlees,
&c. Also, Dr. Nugent, of Dublin.
