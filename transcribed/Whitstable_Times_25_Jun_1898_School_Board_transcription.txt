The attendance sentence, verbatim:
"Legal proceedings were ordered to be taken for irregular attendance against the parents of Edward Price, Albert
Madams, Robert and Arthur Butcher, and Grace Shoesmith; and final warnings are to be served upon the parents of
Edward Castle, Percy Parkinson, Alfred Dunn, Frank and Fred Pout Lucy Parkinson, Mary Ann Castle, and Elizabeth
Acors."

Context in the item (our summary): the June meeting of the Whitstable and Seasalter U.D. School Board, held at the
Clerk's office "on Tuesday evening last" (Tuesday 21 June 1898, by calendar computation), Mr J. W. Pepper in the
chair. The item also reports that the schools had been "closed during the recent flood".

WHAT IT GIVES: one month after the final warning ordered for Albert Madams's parents (Whitstable Times, 21 May 1898,
p. 8: the May meeting, 17 May), the Board ordered legal proceedings against them for his irregular attendance. He
was ten (born 19 June 1887; turned eleven two days before the meeting). Not named: which parent, whether the
proceedings were taken, any result. "Albert Madams": the name the school used, as in May.
NAME, NOTHING TO MAKE OF IT: "Frank and Fred Pout" share the surname of John James Pout, the Harbour Street bootmaker
of the 1901 case. No connection is known or implied.
Wanted: any report of the proceedings (Whitstable or St Augustine's petty sessions, summer 1898).
