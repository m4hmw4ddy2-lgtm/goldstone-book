TRANSCRIPTION

At the September sessions of the Central Criminal Court, which commence to-day, will take place the trial of William Gouldstone, who is charged with murdering five of his children at Walthamstow. The prisoner, it is said, has conducted himself in the most peaceable manner since his committal, but he does not seem at all to appreciate the terrible character of the crime with which he is charged, and it is understood that the defence that will be set up for him will be that of insanity.
