## 1. 1911 CENSUS, 46 EDINBURGH ROAD, CHATHAM: "RENNIE GOLDSTONE" (Clarie Irene, Albert's wife)
Images: sources/images/census-stuff/Clarie Irene 1911 census detail.jpg (6368 x 3744; the schedule) and Clarie Irene
1911 census front.jpg (1500 x 2050; the cover). The first upload of "front" duplicated "detail"; Rik re-uploaded the
cover the same day (now a different file, MD5 c9c6c5dc...). Reference: RG 14/3915, schedule 128 (Ancestry tree; the schedule number
"128" is on the image; the piece is not).
IMAGE CHECK (check-once rule): 22 October 2026 (Claude): the whole schedule read; the row, the nationality cell and the
foot (rooms, signature, address) cropped and enlarged. Certain unless marked.
- Schedule no. "128". Census night Sunday 2 April 1911.
- Row 1: "Rennie Goldstone" | "Wife" | age (female) "23" | "Married" | completed years of present marriage "1" | children
  born alive "None" | personal occupation "Tailoress" (enumerator's code "240") | industry "Tailoring" | "worker" |
  birthplace "Surrey / Kingston on Thames" (red "190") | nationality "Brit sub by Parentage" | infirmity blank.
- Rows 2 to 15 blank. Enumerator's totals: males "-", females "1", persons "1"; initials "G.R.B." [?].
- Rooms: "[3 or 5]. Rooms" (the figure has a flat top: 5 likelier, not certain).
- Signature: "Rennie Goldstone". Postal address: "46 Edinburgh Road".
- COVER (checked 22 October 2026, the enumerator's box cropped and enlarged): Registration District "47"; Sub-District
  "47-1"; Enumeration District "31"; Name of Head of Family or Separate Occupier, in pencil, in the enumerator's space:
  "Mr Goldstone" (read by Rik, 22 October 2026: a capital M with a small raised "r", then "Goldstone" with a long tail;
  Claude's first reading, "[Wm? or Mrs?]", withdrawn). The enumerator named a man as head of the household; the
  schedule itself lists only Rennie, Albert being aboard Dominion that night;
  Postal Address "46 Edinburgh Road", stamped "CHATHAM".
NOTES: her age 23 against a birth on 7 June 1889 (21 on census night): two years over, as in 1910 (22) and 1921 (34)
(Contradiction 91). Albert was aboard Dominion in Glengariff Harbour the same night (his own 1911 transcription).
The first document of the family at 46 Edinburgh Road, their address to at least October 1933 (Observer nominations).
"Brit sub by Parentage" is in a column meant for those born abroad; as written.

## 2. 1939 REGISTER, BATH: "GOLDSTONE, VERA G."
Image: sources/images/census-stuff/Vera Goldstone 1939 Register.jpg (3774 x 4611). Reference: RG 101/7008A (Ancestry
tree; not on the image). Header: E.D. letter code "W.N.B.R."; "BATH C.B."; registration district and sub-district
"316-1". One line of the page is closed ("This record is officially closed").
IMAGE CHECK (check-once rule): 22 October 2026 (Claude): schedule 67 cropped and enlarged; certain unless marked.
- Address "3 Ditto" (the page's previous address is "4 Marlborough St (Contd)", so 3 Marlborough Street), schedule 67:
  1 Cole, Fanny R., F, 7 Oct 70, S, "Independant" [sic]; 2 Mustoo [?], Amy A., F, 22 Sept 89, S, Domestic Servant;
  3 Hussey (struck; "PURFIELD" over it), Freda L., F, 21 Aug 20, S, Civil Servant; 4 Goldstone (struck; "FERGUSON" over
  it), Vera G., F, "1st Mar 21", S, "Civil Servant".
- Margin beside Vera's line: "Cl.20 M [6 or 8]/5/42" (a later annotation of her marriage, dated May 1942; the marriage
  itself was 28 March 1942, certificate).
NOTES: answers live_lists 2 ("Where Vera was at the 1939 Register"): in Bath, a civil servant, boarding with another
young civil servant in a household headed by Fanny R. Cole. Why Bath, or which department, is NOT on the page.
LOCATION (Rik, 22 October 2026, from a modern map): Marlborough Street, Bath, runs north from the junction of Weston Road
and Julian Road, between Cavendish Road (Royal Victoria Park beyond it) and St James's Square. The modern map's house
numbers do not run in a simple order (it shows 11 between 3 and 2), so which building was No. 3 in 1939 is NOT shown;
the Register's own order (4, 3, 2, 1, then 12, 11, 10, 9) is the record. The map image is not in the repo.

## 3. PROBATE CALENDAR (England and Wales), 1980 AND 1982
Images: sources/images/census-stuff/Vera Georgina Ferguson Probate.jpg (1980 calendar, "PAGE 2670"); Lily AR Godbold
Probate.jpg (1982 calendar, "PAGE 3526"). Each entry cropped and enlarged, 22 October 2026 (Claude); every character
certain.
- "FERGUSON, Vera Georgina of 35 Dagmar Rd Chatham Kent died 2 November 1979 Administration London 14 March £13645
  800203211Z" (1980).
- "GODBOLD, Lily Alma Rose of 12 Dornden Gardens Chatham Kent died 2 June 1982 Probate London 5 October Not exceeding
  £25000 820212017P" (1982).
NOTES: Vera's death date (2 November 1979) agrees with the Ancestry tree and dramatis ("November 1979"). Lily's address
at death is 12 Dornden Gardens, not 35 Dagmar Road: dramatis has her moving in with Vera and outliving her; not a
conflict (she may have moved after 1979), but the calendar does not show her at Dagmar Road. Part Six material.

## 4. GRO DEATH INDEX (England and Wales), AS RIK GAVE IT, 22 October 2026 (Ancestry; index text only, no image)
- "Clarie I Goldstone", death age 68, birth "abt 1890" (Ancestry's subtraction), registered Jan-Feb-Mar 1958, Chatham,
  Kent, vol. 5b, p. 369. Age 68 fits her birth on 7 June 1889 for a death in January to March 1958.
  The DAY, 7 January 1958, and the burial at Palmerston Road Cemetery come from the Ancestry tree, whose source for them
  is Find a Grave (Rik's link, Ancestry collection 60526, record 194528623): a secondary index, not the register.
- "George W Ferguson", death age 41, birth "abt 1921" (Ancestry's subtraction), registered Jan-Feb-Mar 1962, Chatham,

  readable from the session: the site refuses automated fetching). The Ancestry tree has death February 1962, buried 15
  February 1962, Palmerston Road Cemetery; MyHeritage's "Mar 1962" is the quarter, not a month.
