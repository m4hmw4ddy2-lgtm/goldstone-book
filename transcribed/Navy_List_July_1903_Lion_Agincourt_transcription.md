## What the entries hold for the book
- LION was an establishment of two ships at Devonport, entered under "Training Ships for Boys": one of 8,223 tons,
  the former Implacable, and one of 3,842 tons, the former Lion; tender, the Liberty. (What the Liberty was is not
  in the entry.)
- AGINCOURT, an armoured first-class cruiser of 10,600 tons, was "Tender to 'Boscawen' as Depôt for Boys" at
  Portland, her people "(Borne in 'Boscawen.')". Albert's sheet nevertheless gives her her own list and book number
  (15c / 16103).
