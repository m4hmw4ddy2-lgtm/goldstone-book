## Compared with the image (Claude, 13 October 2026; crops at 300 dpi)
- Jupiter, first entry: RMG "1st March 1906" is RIGHT; the project's transcription had "11 March 06". Corrected there
  and across the notes.
- Majestic, first entry: RMG "26th December 1904" is WRONG; the sheet reads "20 Dec 04".
- Tyne: RMG "TB 31" is borne out: read "T.B.31" by Rik, 13 October 2026.
- Occupation: RMG gives "Labourer", the word the sheet STRIKES THROUGH; the sheet's standing entry is "Farm Labourer".
- Inferred "To" dates (Prince George 18 June 1904; Lion 27 August 1902; Jupiter 31 December 1907; Tyne 30 June 1916;
  Alexander 31 August 1918 and 16 January 1919): not on the sheet; they are the day before the next entry.
- "Petty Officer (New Series)": the project's expansion of the sheet's "P.O. (ns)" (re-read 13 October 2026; the project's transcription had "(as)", the briefing "(act)"): RMG's reading is borne out.
- Final Pembroke I (17 January to 26 March 1919): blank in RMG, on the sheet.
