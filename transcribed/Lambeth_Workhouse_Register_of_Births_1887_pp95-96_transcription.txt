COUNT (made from the images, 10 October 2026): 17 entries, Saturday 18 June to Friday 8 July 1887 (16 mothers; 106
and 107 are twins). ILLEGITIMATE 11 (103, 104, 105, 106, 107, 108, 110, 112, 113, 114, 119); LEGITIMATE 6 (109, 111,
115, 116, 117, 118). STILLBORN 3 (114, illegitimate; 116 and 118, legitimate): name column ruled through, no
registrar's initials. Entry 110 is the least certain of the legitimacy readings (the split falls inside the word);
read "Illegitimate" from the two tall loops on the right-hand page, against 109's single capital L, and it agrees with
the form of the line (mother's name only, no remark). If 110 were wrong the count would be 10 and 7.

THE FORM OF THE LINES (observed, not explained): every legitimate entry gives both parents, the mother's former
surname ("formerly ...") and, in Remarks, a trade (Coachman, Boiler-maker, Carpenter, Labourer, Boiler Maker, and
one uncertain). Every illegitimate entry gives the mother's name only, no former surname and no remark, except
114's "Stillborn". Albert's line (105) has that form.

Two births on 19 June, both entered illegitimate: 104 (Mary Pratt; a girl, Alice Mary) and 105 (Albert).

NOT IN THIS REGISTER: no informant, no address, no time of birth for 105, no age or occupation for any mother, no
date of registration. The register's entry number (105) is not the civil register's (113, on the GRO certificate).
