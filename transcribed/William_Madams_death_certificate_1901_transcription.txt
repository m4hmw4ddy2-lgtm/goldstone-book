READINGS NOTED:
- Weekdays (calendar computation): died Saturday 23 November; registered Wednesday 27 November 1901.
- Nobody of his family is the informant; the asylum's clerk and steward is.
- Age 42: against the 1891 census (30) and the 1901 census (39), the three ages do not agree with one another or with
  the "born 1 May 1860" carried in dramatis_personae.md (source of that date not recorded). Not for prose.
- "about 3 months": the certificate's duration for the disease as certified. The admission date (4 September 1901,
  MH 94/37) is held only as an index entry (notes/archive/session-addendum-2026-06-25.md; Ancestry tree citation),
  image not in the repo.
- "Chartham", not "Chatham" (live_lists 5). The Ancestry GEDCOM's "Admission to Chatham Asylum" is that error.
