(A) Saturday 12 August 1899, p. 1 (advertisement)

WHITSTABLE. SALE OF FREEHOLD PROPERTY. MR. J. T. REEVES Having been favoured with instructions from the Executors
under the will of the late Mrs. M. A. Amos, WILL SELL BY AUCTION, AT the AUCTION MART, on THURSDAY, the 17th day of
AUGUST, 1899, at 3 o'clock precisely, the following desirable FREEHOLD PROPERTY, In Three Lots as under:
Lot 1.—A Substantial Brick-Built DWELLING-HOUSE, with Shop and Appurtenances thereto, situate and being No. 79, High
Street, Whitstable, and a Timber-built Cottage, in two Tenements, in the rear, let to Mr. C. J. Rose, Watchmaker and
Jeweller, on a lease at the annual rent of £29 16s.
Lot 2.—A Timber-Built MESSUAGE, with Glasshouse, and the Appurtenances thereto, situate and being No. 42, Island
Wall, Whitstable, in the occupation of Mrs. Madams, at the weekly rent of 3s. 3d.

(B) Saturday 19 August 1899, p. 8 (report)

PROPERTY SALES BY MR. J. T. REEVES. On Thursday afternoon last Mr. J. T. Reeves conducted another very successful
sale of property at the Auction Mart, Whitstable. There was a large attendance, and very good prices were realised for
all the lots, which comprised portions of three separate estates. The results were as follows:—Three lots of freehold
property, sold under instructions from the executors under the will of Mrs. A. Amos:—Lot 1, the brick built dwelling
house and shop, No. 79, High Street, Whitstable, and a timber built cottage in two tenements in the rear, let to Mr.
C. J. Rose, on a lease, at the annual rent of £29 16s., was sold to Mr. J. W. Elvin, of Loughborough Park, for £500.
Lot 2, a timber built messuage, with glass house, etc., No. 42, Island Wall, in the occupation of Mrs. Madams at the
weekly rent of 3s. 6d., was purchased by Mr. T. Gill for £105.

NOTES (not for prose without a ruling):
- The rent differs: 3s. 3d. (the notice) and 3s. 6d. (the report). Both certain on the images. One is a misprint;
  which is not known.
- The executor's name differs: "Mrs. M. A. Amos" (notice), "Mrs. A. Amos" (report).
- Sale: Thursday 17 August 1899, the Auction Mart, Whitstable. Seller: the estate of the late Mrs M. A. Amos. Buyer of
  Lot 2: Mr T. Gill, £105.
- WHICH MRS MADAMS is not shown. Elizabeth was "Mrs Madams" in Whitstable (1891 census, 41 Harbour Street; Lilian's
  certificate, 1890, Beach Alley), and in Middle Wall in 1901 (as Goldstone at the police court; as Madams's wife in
  the census). Beach Alley runs between Island Wall and Middle Wall. But William Madams was alive in 1899 (died
  November 1901), and the occupier named is the wife; and other Madams households lived in the town. To settle it:
  the Whitstable rate book or electoral register for Island Wall, 1898-1900; a Whitstable directory of 1899.
- If it is Elizabeth's home, it is the third time the book finds a family's house sold over their heads (the Stocks'
  cottage at Wethersfield, 1861; Thomas Gouldstone's house and smithy, 1867). Noted, not concluded.
