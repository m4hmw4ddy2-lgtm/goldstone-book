Supplied by Rik, 10 October 2026. PAPER AND DATE (Rik, 10 October 2026): Chatham, Rochester and Brompton Observer, Friday 19 October 1934. Page not
recorded. So the election is Thursday 1 November 1934 and "Wednesday next" for nominations is 24 October 1934
(weekdays by calendar computation). Albert was 47 (born 19 June 1887).

IMAGE CHECK (check-once rule): 10 October 2026. Whole item read from the image (the "CHATHAM." paragraphs; the line
above, "...Divisional Labour party.", belongs to the previous item). Not yet quoted anywhere.

The passage on Albert, verbatim:
"Labour will make an effort to capture the seat held in Christ Church ward by Clr. Mrs F. M. Freeman, their chosen
candidate being Mr Albert Goldstone, who has hitherto been unsuccessful in his attempts to secure municipal honours.
Mrs Freeman is popular in the ward by reason of her work, especially in connection with the Maternity and Child
Welfare clinic."

What it gives: an Albert Goldstone stood for Labour in Christ Church ward, Chatham, against a sitting councillor, and
had stood before ("attempts", plural) without being elected.
What it does NOT give: that he was ever a councillor; his address, age or trade; the result of this contest.
Identity: same name and town as Albert Jennet Goldstone; no other Albert Goldstone in Chatham is known to the project,
but nothing on the clip ties it to him beyond the name. Treat as very probably him, to confirm.
