Signature, description and residence of informant: Emily Gouldstone, Mother, Great Sampford [signed, not a mark]
When registered: Twenty ninth May 1873
Signature of registrar: Henry Carslake [read "Caslake"; the "r" is not clear], Registrar

NOTES (not for prose):
- No maiden surname and no father: the form of an unmarried mother's entry. Emily senior was married to Thomas, so the
  mother is very probably the daughter, Emily (19 in April 1871); the 1901 census has Mabel a granddaughter
  (dramatis_personae.md). Both Emilys could write (Emily senior signed William's registration, 1856), so the signature
  does not decide it.
- Henry Carslake: a Mr. Carslake was relieving officer and inspector of nuisances at Great Sampford in 1866 (the
  cholera report, Chelmsford Chronicle, 9 November 1866).
