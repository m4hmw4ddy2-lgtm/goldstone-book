TRANSCRIPTION

MURDER OF FOUR CHILDREN.

Last night, at about half-past 5 or 6 o'clock, a shocking crime was perpetrated at St. James's-street,
Walthamstow, a father having drowned three of his children—Charles, aged 3½ years; Herbert, 2½ years; and
Frederick, aged 16 months—and so seriously assaulted two others—twin boys only seven days old—that one of them died
shortly afterwards, and the death of the second was hourly expected. It seems that at No. 8, Courtney-place-terrace,
in a small courtway near St. James's-street Station, there resided a blacksmith named William Gouldstone, aged 43,
who was employed by a firm in Lower Thames street, City. Last night week his wife was confined of twins, and she
had a woman in the house to nurse her. About 6 o'clock last evening Gouldstone returned home, rather earlier than
usual. He seemed in a very bad mood, and said to the nurse that he wanted her out of the house while he had a chat
with his wife concerning the medical expenses of the confinement. The woman went out of the house, and just before
going she heard the father ask for the boy Frederick. The woman was surprised a short time afterwards at seeing the
cistern running over, and on going to it she was horrified to discover the dead bodies of three children in it. At
the same moment she heard screaming in the room where Mrs. Gouldstone was in bed, and she knocked at the door, but
was unable to gain entrance. She called for assistance, and the door was burst open. Then it was found that the
enraged father, after drowning his three eldest children, had struck the twin babes at their mother's breast with
a hammer. The police were at once sent for, and the man was taken into custody and conveyed to the Lea-bridge-road
Police Station. When the nurse asked the prisoner what he was doing he said, "Never mind, I am happy now, and she
is a single woman." No motive can be assigned for the murders, except one—viz., that the prisoner has on more than
one occasion quarrelled, or rather grumbled, with his wife because she had children so quickly. The poor woman
was, as has been already stated, only confined last week. Since that time the prisoner has not spoken to her. He
is stated to be a most abstemious man, and has been in his present employ five or six years. He will be brought up
at the Stratford Petty Sessions to-day.
