OLD SAMPFORD.—STRIKE OF LABOURERS.—Rather than submit to have their wages reduced from 12s. to 11s. per week, a large
number labourers' of this place are on strike. The National Agricultural Labourers' Union pay such as are members 9s.
per week. It is feared that another batch will shortly be on strike, as it has been intimated to the men that their
money must be reduced for the present or for a few weeks. They, however, do not feel inclined to submit to any
reduction. Those who are on strike behave themselves remarkably well at present.

NOTE: "a large number labourers' of this place" is the printer's (sic); paraphrase round it.
