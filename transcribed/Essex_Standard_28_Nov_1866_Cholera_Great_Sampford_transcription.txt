THE CHOLERA OUTBREAK AT GREAT SAMPFORD.—It is some satisfaction to learn that, although there has been, as reported,
an outbreak of Cholera at Great Sampford, the details given of its ravages have been very much exaggerated. There were
7 or 8 deaths, but the number of attacks, instead of approaching 40, did not, we are assured, exceed 18; and for many
days past there has not been a single fresh case, so that the visitation, it is hoped, is at an end.

NOTES: "approaching 40" answers a report not yet found (not the Chelmsford Chronicle of 23 November, which says
"about 18 cases"). Under PRESS AS PRESS, the papers' figures stay the papers'.
