TRANSFER of Food Registrations.—In consequence of a change of ownership, persons registered with James & Aspinall,
155 Dale St., Chatham, for rationed foods will have their registrations automatically transferred to the new
proprietor, A. Goldstone, 155, Dale St Chatham, unless application for transfer to another trader is made at the Food
Office before the 17th Nov., 1945.—Announcement by the Ministry of Food.

NOTES: NEW. A shop at 155 Dale Street, Chatham, registered for rationed foods (so a grocer's or similar), changed hands
in the autumn of 1945; the new proprietor was "A. Goldstone". Which A. Goldstone is NOT shown: Albert (then 58 or 59)
is possible but unproven; the 1939 Register has his daughter Clarie Irene as a grocer, and the shop could be the
family's under his initial. Needs a second source (the 1946 or 1947 Kelly's directory for Chatham, or the electoral
register for Dale Street) before anything is drawn.
