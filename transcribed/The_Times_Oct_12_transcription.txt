TRANSCRIPTION

[...GOU]LDSTONE.—Wm. Graves, [for himself and fellow work]men, begs gratefully to ACKNOWLEDGE the RECEIPT of the
SUM of £35 14s. 4d., remitted to the Rev. T. H. Grantham Robinson, Vicar of Walthamstow, for the defence of Wm.
Gouldstone. This, the total amount subscribed, is insufficient to meet the bare expenses of the trial, and
therefore no provision can be made for the poor woman who has lost her five children, and whose husband and sole
support is now taken from her.

Subscriptions are earnestly solicited, and will be thankfully received by the Rev. T. H. Grantham-Robinson, St.
Saviour's Vicarage, Walthamstow; or by the above-named Wm. Graves, foreman, 67, Upper Thames-street, E.C.
