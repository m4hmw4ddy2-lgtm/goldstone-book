INSOLVENT DEBTORS.—At the Court held on Friday last, the following Insolvents were brought up:—Henry Hammond, of
Hatfield Broad Oak, coal-carter, opposed, entitled to his discharge at the expiration of six months from filing his
petition.—William Rusbrook, of Ballingdon, labourer, opposed, entitled to his discharge conditionally on filing a copy
of lease, otherwise adjourned to the next circuit.—James Johnson, Great Holland, jobber, entitled to his discharge
conditionally on proving his discharge for first contempt of court, otherwise adjourned to next circuit.—Joseph Smyth,
Romford, colt-breaker, discharged.—Samuel Brown, Heron Gate, harness-maker, discharged.—The following Insolvents were
also examined, and, not being opposed, were discharged:—John Saward, formerly of Bermondsey, but now of Widford,
tanner; Joseph Gouldstone, of Great Sampford, blacksmith; Samuel Gooch, jun., of Chelmsford, veterinary-surgeon;
Robert Gunn, of Chigwell, carpenter; Edward Adams, of Elmstead, farmer; Samuel Poulten, of Romford, glazier; and
Isaac Alexander, of Manningtree, coachmaker.

NOTES: "Friday last" in the issue of Friday 8 March 1839 = Friday 1 March 1839.
