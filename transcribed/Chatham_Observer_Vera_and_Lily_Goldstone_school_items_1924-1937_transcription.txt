SCHOOL ITEMS NAMING LILY A. R. GOLDSTONE (1924) AND VERA GOLDSTONE (1931-1937), CHATHAM, ROCHESTER AND BROMPTON
OBSERVER. Rik's clips, 20 October 2026 (sources/images/Media-coverage/Part-Five/, filenames below); dates and pages
from Findmypast.
Transcription scope: the sentence or list entry naming the Goldstone girl in each, with the article's heading; the
rest of each article not transcribed.
IMAGE CHECK (check-once rule): 20 October 2026, each quoted passage from a crop enlarged twice, and each heading from
the whole clip. Every word certain. End-of-line hyphens closed up.
FURTHER READING, 29 October 2026 (Claude), for the Ch21 amendment: items 2, 4 and 5 read whole from their clips. Item 2:
the event was "on Friday" (18 December 1931); Laidman Browne (Rochester Repertory Players) as Father Christmas, Mr Robert
Roberts as Mickey Mouse; the form prizes "distributed" by the Rector of Luton, the list beginning "Class 1A, Vera
Goldstone". Item 4: the school is not named on the clip; the report lists Royal Society of Arts passes (typewriting,
book-keeping, Spanish, shorthand), "Civil Service examinations", "OLD GIRLS' SUCCESSES", and ends with the evening's
items. Item 5: the play's title is not printed as such; "Joan Kay as Lady Precious Stream" (the title part), "As the hero,
Hsieh Ping-Kui, Sylvia Cox", "Molly Purchase ... Wei, the Tiger General", "this lovely play is perhaps their best
production", "there being no scenery". The play is S. I. Hsiung's "Lady Precious Stream" (general knowledge; the
characters named match). Sylvia Cox and Molly Purchase appear in both items 4 and 5: support (not proof) that item 4
is the County School's.

(1) Friday 1 August 1924, p. 8 (…_01_August_1924_0008_Clip.jpg). "Local Scholarships." "At the meeting of the
Medway Education Board on Monday it was announced that scholarships had been awarded to the following:—CHATHAM
COUNTY SCHOOL FOR GIRLS. Free places tenable as from September next:—" [list] "… Lily A. R. Goldstone, Luton Council;
…"

(2) Friday 25 December 1931, p. 2 (…_25_December_1931_0002_Clip.jpg). "Repertory Player Becomes Father Christmas.
DELIGHTS FOR LUTON GIRLS AND INFANTS." At Luton Junior and Infants' Council School: "the Rector of Luton (Rev. A. E.
Blake) distributed form prizes to the following girls and boy, which had been given by Mr William Crittenden,
sen.:—Class 1A, Vera Goldstone; 1B, Lilian Eacott; …"

(3) Friday 24 June 1932, p. 11 (…_24_June_1932_0011_Clip.jpg). "A COVETED HEADMASTERSHIP." Subhead "SCHOLARSHIPS FOR
BOYS AND GIRLS." "The award of the following scholarships offered by the Chatham Education Committee was
announced:—Chatham County School for Girls—D. Brown, Dorothy Jenner (Glencoe-road), J. Cox (All Saints'), Vera
Goldstone and Sylvia Wragg (Luton Council), Marjorie Stone and Marjorie Stowell (Ordnance-street)."

(4) Friday 9 November 1934, p. 7 (…_09_November_1934_0007_Clip.jpg). A school prize-giving: lists of Royal Society of
Arts examinations, 1934, Civil Service examinations and "OLD GIRLS' SUCCESSES" (the school is not named on the clip;
by the names and the later items, the County School for Girls). "During the evening there were vocal and choral
speaking items by the various forms and individual items by Vera Goldstone, Sylvia Cox and Molly Purchase."

(5) Friday 27 March 1936, p. 7 (…_27_March_1936_0007_Clip.jpg). "A SEVERE TEST." "The girls of Chatham County School
have a very high reputation as amateur players to maintain, and this lovely play is perhaps their best production"
(the play: "Lady Precious Stream", by the parts named). "… Vera Goldstone acted with charm and conviction in the part
of the Prime Minister's wife."

(6) Friday 11 June 1937, p. 9 (…_11_June_1937_0009_Clip.jpg). "CIVIL SERVICE EXAM. SUCCESSES." "In the recent
examination held by the Civil Service Commissioners the following pupils of the County School for Girls, Chatham, have
been successful and will be offered posts: Clerical grade—…. Clerical assistant grade—Betty Ames, Janet Bennett, Vera
Goldstone, Eileen Kersey, …"

(7) Friday 30 July 1937, p. 10 (…_30_July_1937_0010_Clip.jpg; a second copy, "…Clip (1).jpg", is the same image).
"County School for Girls." "The following pupils of the County School for Girls, Chatham, were successful in the
University of London general school and matriculation examination for school and matriculation certificate: … Vera
Goldstone, v.g. spoken French; …" (no "mat.", so a school certificate, not matriculation).

IDENTIFICATION (notes; for Rik):
- (1) "Lily A. R. Goldstone, Luton Council": the three initials are Lily Alma Rose Goldstone's (born 14 December 1913,
  1939 Register), of an age to take a free place at eleven in September 1924, and Luton Council School is the school
  of Albert's district (Luton, Chatham; the family at 46 Edinburgh Road in 1921). Very probably Albert's daughter, the
  "Auntie Lil" of family memory.
- (2) to (7) "Vera Goldstone": Vera Georgina Goldstone, born 1 March 1921 (Rik's grandmother), would be 10 at
  Christmas 1931 (Class 1A of the junior school), 11 for a County School scholarship in June 1932, 16 in summer 1937
  (school certificate; Civil Service clerical assistant). Both sisters went from Luton Council School to the County
  School for Girls on a free place or scholarship. The series fits one girl and her ages throughout; no item names
  her parents or address. Very probable, not proven. (The 1939 Register's Lily as "civil servant" and Vera's Civil
  Service pass of 1937 may be compared.)
