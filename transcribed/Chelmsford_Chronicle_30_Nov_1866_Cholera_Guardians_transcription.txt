CHOLERA AT GREAT SAMPFORD.—At a meeting of the Board of Guardians of the Saffron Walden union, held on November 23rd,
it was resolved that the guardians had great pleasure in expres[s]ing to Mr. Fry their estimation of his attention,
assiduity, and medical skill in treating and arresting the progress of the cholera, which so unfortunately occurred
in the said parish.
