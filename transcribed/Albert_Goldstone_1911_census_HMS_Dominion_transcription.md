# Albert Goldstone, 1911 census, HMS Dominion: transcription of his row

**Images (sources/images/census-stuff/):** the enumeration book of HMS Dominion, four pages held:
- `Albert Goldstone 1911 census Cover Sheet.jpg`: cover, page i.
- `Albert Goldstone 1911 census p2.jpg`: page ii, Tables 1 to 3 (totals).
- `Albert Goldstone 1911 census Cover Sheet p3 senior officers.jpg`: page 1, the officers (rows 1-20).
- `Albert Goldstone 1911 census.jpg`: page 11, Albert's page (row 22).
Reference by the Ancestry index: RG 14/34971 (not on the images). The service sheet has him in Dominion from 7 June
1910 to 23 May 1911 (ADM 188/383, seq. 18).

**IMAGE CHECK, 13 October 2026 (Claude): every word of row 22 and the page-11 heading, and every handwritten entry on
the cover, cropped and enlarged, compared against the images.** One letter left uncertain by Claude, settled by Rik the same day (below). Page ii's totals read at
full-page size only. Page 1 read for the heading and the captain's row only; the other rows on pages 1 and 11 were not
transcribed.

## Cover, page i (printed, with the handwritten entries in bold)
CENSUS OF ENGLAND AND WALES, 1911. / ENUMERATION BOOK FOR THE ROYAL NAVY.
- His Majesty's Ship: **"Dominion"**
- Class and denomination of Ship: **Battleship, 1st class.** [the "st" superscript]
- Name of Captain or Commanding Officer: **Morgan Singer, Captain**
- Station: **2nd Division Home Fleet** [the "nd" superscript]
- Position of the Ship at midnight on Sunday, April 2nd, 1911: **Glengariff Harbour, Ireland.**

## Page ii, totals (full-page reading)
Table 2, at midnight on 2 April 1911, on board: commissioned officers 23; warrant officers 10; other officers 1;
seamen 61[?] (the second digit overwritten); boys 6; Royal Marines 113; total on ship's books 764. Not on board
(Table 3): 6 (seamen 4, Royal Marines 2). Table 1: 764 on the ship's books on board, and 5 "Passengers and all other
persons not on the ship's books", a total of 769 on board. Signed "Morgan Singer", Captain.

## Page 1 (the officers)
Heading: "List of Officers, Crew and Royal Marines on board His Majesty's Ship Dominion. at midnight on Sunday, April
2nd, 1911." Row 1: Morgan Singer, 46, married, Captain, Executive, born Woolwich, Kent.

## Page 11, heading (printed)
List of Officers, Crew, and Royal Marines on Board at midnight on Sunday, April 2nd, 1911. [page] 11

## Notes
- AGE 24 on 2 April 1911 fits the naval 1886 (born 19 June 1886: 24), not his birth certificate's 1887 (23). The
  1911 entry carries the false year as well as the false birthplace.
- "Honyllon": read by Rik against the image, 13 October 2026 (Claude had left the fourth letter open as [y/g]; the Ancestry index also reads "Honyllon").
- The spelling differs from the service sheet's "Heckenning Houzellon" (1901) and from the 1921 census.
- The ship's position comes from the cover: in Glengariff Harbour, Ireland, at midnight on 2 April 1911.
- Page 11 total: 29 (row 6, Ernest Woodward, struck through).
