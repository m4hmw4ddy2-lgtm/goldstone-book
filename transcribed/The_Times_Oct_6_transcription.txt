TRANSCRIPTION

THE CONVICT GOULDSTONE.

The solicitor of the convict Gouldstone's employers has received the following letter from the Home Office:—
"Whitehall, Oct. 3, 1883,—Sir,—With reference to the applications forwarded by you in behalf of William
Gouldstone, I am directed by the Secretary of State to acquaint you that, having caused special medical inquiry to
be made as to the state of this man's mind, he has felt justified in advising Her Majesty to respite the capital
sentence.—I am, Sir, your obedient servant, GODFREY LUSHINGTON."

Gouldstone has been visited this week by his wife and his sister. It is expected that he will shortly be removed
to Broadmoor Criminal Lunatic Asylum.
