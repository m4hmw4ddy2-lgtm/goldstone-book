READINGS NOTED:
- The register gives the birth date, 7 April 1890, as stated at the baptism. This is the first contemporary
  document for it (before: family tree only). It is not a birth certificate: no place of birth, no informant.
- Saturday 17 October 1891 (calendar computation): five weeks after Eliza Jane's birth (16 September) and twelve days
  before Elizabeth registered her (Thursday 29 October). Eliza Jane WAS baptised the same day: No. 833
  (transcribed/Eliza_Jane_Madams_baptism_1891_transcription.txt).
- The father's trade is "Coal Porter" here; "General Labourer" on the 1891 census and on Eliza Jane's certificate.
- The register enters the parents under the one surname, "Madams".
