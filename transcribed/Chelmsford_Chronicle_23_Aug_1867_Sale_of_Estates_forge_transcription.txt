SALE OF ESTATES.

The following property in the parishes of Hempsted, Radwinter, Wimbish, Old Sampford, Otten and Walter Belchamp, was
offered by auction by Mr. WILLIAM RAND, at the Rose and Crown Hotel, Saffron Walden, on the 16th inst.:—

Lot 1. Anser Field and two cottages and gardens, Hempsted, together 4a. 2r. 31p.—Mr. Snape, £305.
Lot 2. Freehold farm in occupation of Mr. E. Andrews, 21a. 2r. 32p.—Not sold.
Lot 3. Freehold arable land called Long Croft, 7a. 2r. 30p.—Messrs. Hawkes, £380.
Lot 4. Four freehold tenements with outbuildings and gardens, area 1r. 25p., in occupation of Messrs. Halls, Moss,
and others.—Not sold.
Lot 5. Ditto, occupied by Messrs. Robinson, J. Hales, and others.—Not sold.
Lot 6. Two freehold tenements with large gardens, occupied by E. Purkiss and J. Halls.—Mr. G. Surridge, £80.
Lot 7. Ditto, with garden at back, in occupation of W. Battle and J. Mortlock.—Mr. Jas. Archer, £125.
Lot 8. Ditto, with large garden, in occupation of R. Sorrel and R. Willis.—Mr. W. Willis, £70.
Lot 9. Freehold house with wheelwright's shop, yard, and garden, occupied by Mr. Betts.—Mr. Betts, £152.
Lot 10. Freehold farm, Cracknells, 14a. 1r. 1p., in occupation of Mr. E. Andrews.—Mr. R. Medcalf, £820.
Lot 11. Freehold cottage and orchard in occupation of Mr. Geo. Willis.—Mr. W. Willis, £58.
Lot 12. Freehold residence with carpenter's shop, outbuildings, yard, and garden, in occupation of J. Stubbings.—Mr.
J. Stubbings, £210.
Lot 13. Freehold arable and grass land, 5a. 2r. 18p., in occupation of Mr. E. Andrews, and a cottage occupied by
George Willis.—Mr. Chapman, £310.
Lot 14. Freehold arable land, "Great and Little Barrels," 5a. 1r. 18p., in occupation of Mr. E. Andrews.—Mr. E.
Andrews, £270.
Lot 15. Two freehold tenements, in occupation of J. Willis and Mrs. Mortlock.—Mr. Brook, £88.
Lot 16. Blagdens, 112a. 1r. 20p. of arable and grass land, with house, homestead, &c., in occupation of Mr. Jno.
Snape.—Not sold.
Lot 17. Rent-charge of £70 per annum from lands in Hempsted.—Mr. W P. Chalk, £1,650.
Lot 18. Richmond's Farm, Radwinter, consisting of house, homestead, two cottages, and 55a. 3r. 7p. of arable and grass
land, in the occupation of Mr. R. H. Bedford.—Not sold.
Lot 19. Field, arable, 3a. 0r. 24p., in occupation of Mr. Bedford.—Mr. J. Gowlett, £125.
Lot 20. Ditto and three tenements, the whole 1a. 1r. 19p., in occupation of J. Freeman and others.—Mr. J. Gowlet,
£175.
Lot 21. Arable land and a cottage and garden, in occupation of J. Chapman and J. Bacon, 6a. 2r. 13p.—J. Cutts, Esq.,
£350.
Lot 22. The Maypole Farm, Wimbish, containing 115a. 1r. 14p., with house and homestead, in occupation of Mrs.
Norris.—Not sold.
Lot 23. Stettles Farm, Otten and Walter Belchamp, residence homestead, and 124a. 2r. 20p., let to Mr. Thos.
Edwards.—Not sold.
Lot 24. Two freehold tenements with garden, let to Mr. Thos. Edwards.—Not sold.
Lot 25. Two double tenements, in Old Sampford, in occupation of A. Willison and others.—Mr. A. Willison, £152.
Lot 26. Freehold house and blacksmith's shop, &c., in occupation of T. Gouldstone.—Mr. G. Wright, £102.
Lot 27. Ditto, with carpenter's shop, &c., paddock at back, 2a. 0r. 3p., in occupation of Geo. Grey.—Mr. Geo. Grey,
£270.
Lot 28. Freehold messuage, in occupation of S. Porter and his undertenant.—Mr. Geo. Grey, £74.
Lot 29. Freehold house and wheelwright's shop, let to Mr. Phillpott.—Mr. W. Willis, £100.
Lot 30. Freehold double tenement, in occupation of H. Cornell and Wright.—Mr. Goodwin, £70.
Lot 31. Three freehold tenements, in occupation of W. Jeffery and others.—Mr. W. Willis, £107.
Lot 32. Freehold garden ground, The Orchard, area 1r. 34p., in occupation of T. Gouldstone and W. Phillpott.—Mr.
Cutts, £58.
Lot 33. Two £25 shares in Saffron Walden Corn Exchange Company.—Mr. Jno. Clarke, £44.

NOTES (not for prose without a ruling):
- "Old Sampford" is the auction's name for Great Sampford (as in the directories: "Gouldstone T. Old Sampford").
- Lot 26: Thomas was the occupier; the buyer, "Mr. G. Wright", is listed as a blacksmith at Great Sampford in the
  Post Office Directory of 1871 (sources/images/directories/GB0653-00190.jpg). A "Wright" was also a tenant in Lot 30.
- Lot 27, next lot, "Ditto, with carpenter's shop": the occupier and buyer George Grey, carpenter (the Gray family of
  the 1866 cholera report, "a carpenter's son ... named Gray": spelling differs).
- Lot 2, 10, 13, 14: "Mr. E. Andrews", a farmer in the parishes of the sale. No link to Emily's sister's husband
  Thomas Andrews is drawn.
