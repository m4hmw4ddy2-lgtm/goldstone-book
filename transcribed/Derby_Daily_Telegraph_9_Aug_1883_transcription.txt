TRANSCRIPTION

A blacksmith named William Gouldstone, living at Walthamstow, yesterday drowned three of his children in a water butt
and afterwards attacked his twin infants, killing one and mortally injuring the other. The cares of a large and
growing family are believed to have upset his mind.

TRANSCRIBER'S NOTES

1. NOT the wire dispatch of 9 August (Western Daily Press, Nottingham, Middlesbrough). A separate, much shorter
   telegram: no nurse, no hammer, no cistern ("a water butt"), and none of the dispatch's invented detail.
2. "The cares of a large and growing family are believed to have upset his mind": the first printed motive, on the
   morning after, and the first printed suggestion of a disordered mind. Press, unattributed.
3. Written during the night of 8 to 9 August: one twin dead, the other "mortally" injured, as in the wire dispatch.
