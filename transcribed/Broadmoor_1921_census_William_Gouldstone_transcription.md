# 1921 CENSUS, BROADMOOR CRIMINAL LUNATIC ASYLUM, CROWTHORNE: WILLIAM GOULDSTONE (ROW 10 OF THE PAGE HELD)

Image: sources/images/broadmoor-documents/1921 census Broadmoor.jpg (6492 x 3012; a single institution schedule page,
"Page 1."). Reference (RG 15 piece, schedule) NOT on the image and NOT recorded in the repo: to add.
Transcription created 22 October 2026 (Chapter Twenty-One planning, CHAPTER_21_skeleton.md); none existed before.
master-index.md still lists the image as UNREAD (correction proposed in the skeleton, not applied).

IMAGE CHECK (check-once rule): 22 October 2026 (Claude). The page read whole at reduced size; row 10 cropped and
enlarged (columns a to f), every word certain. The other nine rows are other men and are NOT transcribed.

Form: the 1921 institution schedule, printed for "the night of Sunday, 24th April, 1921" (the census was taken on
19 June 1921; the form's printed date is not the census night: see the dates table, 19 Jun 1921).
Written across the occupation columns of rows 2 to 4, one entry for the page: "Inmates of Broadmoor / Criminal Lunatic
Asylum / Crowthorne Berks".

Row 10, as written:
- (a) Name: "William Gouldstone"
- (b) Relationship: "Do" (ditto: "Inmate", row 1)
- (c) Age: "64" years; months: "." (a dot; rows 1 to 6 and 8 have "?")
- (d) Sex: "M"
- (e) Marriage: "Married"
- (f) Birthplace: "Essex" / "Gt. Sanford" [sic, for Great Sampford]
- (g) to (o): blank. In the margin before the row number, the enumerator's green "3" (as on every row); beneath the
  page, in pencil, "M. 10".

NOTES (for Rik; not prose):
- Age 64 is right for his birth on 10 October 1856 (birth certificate): 64 on 19 June 1921.
- "Married", in 1921, fifteen years after Elizabeth's death (29 June 1906). Milne's deposition of 1935 also has "a
  married man"; only the typed inquest slip of 1935 has "Widower". So no Broadmoor document held shows the asylum
  knowing of her death. See CHAPTER_21_skeleton.md, findings, and the proposed contradiction there.
- The same census night as Albert's own schedule at 46 Edinburgh Road, Chatham (RG 15/04062, schedule 109).
  Nothing joins the two documents, and no sentence of ours is to (PART_FIVE_plan.md, DECIDE 7 and 9).
