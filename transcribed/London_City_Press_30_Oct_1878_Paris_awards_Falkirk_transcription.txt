PARIS EXHIBITION.—AWARDS TO LONDON FIRMS.
A FIFTH list of awards made by the International Jury to London exhibitors is here presented to our readers. ...
[Group III.—FURNITURE AND ACCESSORIES.]
...
Reigo, Mr. Michael, 284, Regent-street. Bronze Medal, Class 26.
Falkirk Iron Company, 67, Upper Thames-street. Honourable Mention, Class 27.
Gardner J., and Sons, Messrs., 453, Strand. Bronze Medal, Class 27: Lamps, oil and petroleum; also Honourable Mention,
Class 36, for embroidery.
Rosser and Russell, Messrs. 7, Charing-cross. Silver Medal, Class 27: Heating apparatus.

NOTES: what Class 27 covered is not stated for the Falkirk entry; on the same page Class 27 includes "Lamps, oil and
petroleum" and "Heating apparatus". Any wider statement about the class needs its own source.
