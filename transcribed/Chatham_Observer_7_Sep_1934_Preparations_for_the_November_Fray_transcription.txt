The Goldstone passage, verbatim:
"Another chosen Labour candidate is Mr. Albert Goldstone, who last year was beaten by 35 votes in a close contest
with Clr. G. B. Silver, in Luton Ward. In the forthcoming contest he will be joining issue with Clr. Mrs. F. M.
Freeman, who has held a seat in Christ Church Ward since 1928."

WHAT IT GIVES: by early September 1934 Labour had chosen him for Christ Church ward; the 1933 Luton result confirmed
(beaten by 35 by G. B. Silver: agrees with the Observer of 3 November 1933); Freeman a councillor for Christ Church
since 1928. It names no contest of his before 1933, though the Observer of 19 October 1934 says "attempts".
PRESS DISCREPANCY: the Luton councillor is "Clr. R. O. Harris" here, "Clr. F. O. Harris" on 19 October 1934, and
"*Harris, Franklin Osborne (C.)" in the returns of 2 November 1934. The returns are the authority; "R. O." is this
paper's slip. Nothing in the book turns on it.
