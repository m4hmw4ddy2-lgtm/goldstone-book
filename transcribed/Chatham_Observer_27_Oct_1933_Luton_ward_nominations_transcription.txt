Text, verbatim:
"LUTON WARD.
GOLDSTONE, Albert (Labour), 46, Edinburgh-road, Chatham, ship rigger in H.M. Dockyard; proposed by Robert Elliott
Russell, seconded by William Thomas McCully.
*SILVER, George Brown (Conservative), Watling Tennis Courts, Star Mill-lane, Chatham, builder and contractor,
proposed by Sidney James Elcomb, seconded by Gordon Fowle."

WHAT IT GIVES: the candidate's address and trade. "46, Edinburgh-road" is the address of Albert Jennet Goldstone's
household in the 1921 census (RG 15/04062, schedule 109) and on Vera's birth certificate (1921); "ship rigger" is his
trade in the 1939 Register. This ties the Labour candidate of 1933 (and with the 1945 club report, of 1934) to him
by address and trade, not by name alone. Identity now SETTLED.
