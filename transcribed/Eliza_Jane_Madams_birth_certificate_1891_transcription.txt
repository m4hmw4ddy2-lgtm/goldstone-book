Margin (left of the entry): "Four" / "E.J." [meaning not known; not for prose]
No.: 177
1 When and where born: "Sixteenth [Oct struck through] September 1891 Harbour Place Whitstable U.S.D."
  (The clerk first wrote "Oct", struck it through, and wrote "September". Nothing to make of it.)
2 Name, if any: "Eliza Jane"
3 Sex: "Girl"
4 Name and surname of father: "William Madams"
5 Name, surname and maiden surname of mother: "Elizabeth Madams late Goldstone formerly Stock"
6 Occupation of father: "General Labourer"

8 When registered: "Twenty ninth October 1891"
9 Signature of registrar: "Edwin Johnson Registrar"
10 Name entered after registration: (blank; struck through)

READINGS NOTED:
- Elizabeth is the informant. The copy is in the copying clerk's hand, so whether she signed or made a mark cannot be
  told (no "X the mark of" appears on the copy, but a certified copy need not reproduce one; nothing to be made of
  it either way).
- Weekdays (calendar computation): born Wednesday 16 September; registered Thursday 29 October 1891, forty-three
  days later. The legal period for registration is general knowledge outside the repo: NOT for prose.
- "Goldstone", with the e, here; "Goldston" on Albert's certificate (1887). Spellings carry no meaning (Rik). The
  maiden surname "Stock" is given here and NOT on Albert's certificate (CHAPTER_18_skeleton.md, finding 5, DECIDE D).
- The same registrar, Edwin Johnson, registered her death in 1906.
