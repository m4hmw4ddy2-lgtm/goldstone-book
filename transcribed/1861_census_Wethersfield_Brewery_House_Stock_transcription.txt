Notes:
- Thomas Stock and Jemima are both given as born at Wethersfield. Thomas was baptised at Finchingfield (1802) and
  Jemima at Hornchurch (1811): the census birthplaces are wrong for both. Not for the prose.
- Thomas's 54 and Jemima's 49: as in MASTER_NOTES ("Final corrections"); Jemima's 49 fits her 1811 baptism.
- The 1861 sale (Chelmsford Chronicle, 30 August 1861) has the double tenement "in the occupation of Thomas Stock
  and Harriet Love". No Harriet is on this page; Elizabeth Love (schedule 16) is the nearest. Not resolved.
