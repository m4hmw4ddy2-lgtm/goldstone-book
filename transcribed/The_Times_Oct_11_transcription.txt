NOTE: "Yesterday morning" is Wednesday 10 October. The Preston Chronicle of 13 October (held) has the same report
("received on Wednesday morning"). This bears on Contradiction 80 (the day the Sheriff and William were told:

that the capital sentence would not be carried out both arrived on 10 October, and the Under Sheriff, Charles
Gepp, told him "shortly after the receipt of the letter".

TRANSCRIPTION

THE CONVICT GOULDSTONE.

Yesterday morning the High Sheriff of Essex received a communication from the Home Office stating that the
convict William Gouldstone, convicted of the murder of his five children at Walthamstow, had been certified to be
of unsound mind, and that he will be removed to Broadmoor Criminal Lunatic Asylum as soon as the necessary
documents can be completed. It was added that it might be intimated to Gouldstone that the capital sentence would
not be carried out. This intimation was accordingly made to the convict by the Under Sheriff, Mr. Charles Gepp,
shortly after the receipt of the letter, and Gouldstone received it with deep gratitude.
