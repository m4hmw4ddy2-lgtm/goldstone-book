# Carswell (ed.), Trial of Ronald True (Notable British Trials), p. 47: "Leading Dates in the Ronald True Case"

**Source:** Donald Carswell (ed.), *Trial of Ronald True*, Notable British Trials (Edinburgh and London: William
Hodge). EDITION NOT STATED on the page held (first edition 1925; new edition 1950): TO RECORD. Page 47 (number at the
foot). Image: sources/pdfs/Carswell_Trial_of_Ronald_True_p47_Leading_Dates.png (Rik's screenshot, 27 October 2026).

**Scope:** the 1922 entries only (the rows that bear on Chapter Twenty-Two). Earlier rows (1891-1921) not transcribed.

**TRANSCRIBED FROM THE IMAGE, 27 October 2026 (Claude):** every word of the rows below read against the image; legible
throughout. The year in the first 1922 row is printed with a damaged final figure ("192?"); the rows that follow are
ditto marks under it, and the sequence fixes it as 1922.

**Status:** an editor's chronology in a published trial volume (secondary to the court record, but the standard
printed account). Wikipedia's dates for the conviction (5 May), appeal (25 May) and reprieve (8 June) agree with it.

## Notes for the chapter (Claude, 27 October 2026; not part of the transcription)
- The chronology joins respite and removal on one day: "8th June ... Sentence respited on the ground of insanity.
  True removed to Broadmoor." Whether he physically reached Broadmoor that day is not established by this entry
  (William's removal took a week: warrants 8 October, admission 15 October 1883). Prose: "reprieved on the eighth of
  June and sent to Broadmoor", not "arrived that day".
- The chronology calls it "respited", the same word as William's case in 1883.
- The victim appears as "Gertrude Yates ('Olive Young')".

## TRANSCRIPTION (1922 rows)

1922. Feb. (Mid.), - Left home and began wandering about the West-End of London. Became acquainted with Gertrude Yates
("Olive Young").
" 5-6th March, Murder of "Olive Young."
" 6th ", True arrested at Hammersmith Palace of Varieties.
" 1st May, - Trial at Central Criminal Court begun.
" 5th ", - Found guilty and sentenced to death.
" 25th ", - Appeal dismissed.
" 8th June, - Sentence respited on the ground of insanity. True removed to Broadmoor.
