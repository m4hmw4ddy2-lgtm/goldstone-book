ELECTION CANDIDATES.

As previously announced, Mr A. Goldstone was chosen by the Labour party as a candidate to oppose Clr. Mark Packer in
Christ Church Ward, but as the latter has since become Mayor-elect Mr Goldstone will contest Luton ward, where he will
be in opposition to Clr. G. B. Silver.
