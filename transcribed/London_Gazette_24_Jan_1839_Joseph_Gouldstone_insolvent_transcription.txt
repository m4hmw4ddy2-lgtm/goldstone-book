Joseph Gouldstone, late of Great Sampford, Essex, Blacksmith.—In the Gaol of Chelmsford.

NOTES: Joseph (baptised 1793) was William's grandfather. "On their own Petitions": he petitioned the court himself
from the gaol. His discharge: Essex Standard, 8 March 1839 (its transcription). Thomas, his son, was seventeen.
