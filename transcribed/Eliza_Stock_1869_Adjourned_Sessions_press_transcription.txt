Text, verbatim:
"PLEADED GUILTY.
Eliza Stock (17), servant, pleaded guilty to stealing a silk jacket, several pieces of lace, a veil, and several
other articles, the property of Alfred Snow, at Great Dunmow, on Jan. 25th.—Six weeks' hard labour.
The business of the Session terminated at 1.45 p.m."

Matches the HO 140/5 index (Findmypast) on name, occupation and victim; the index gives the trial as Chelmsford,
Adjourned Sessions, 16 February 1869, so "Jan. 25th" is 25 January 1869 (a Monday, by calendar computation).

AGES DO NOT AGREE: the calendar index gives 15; this report 17. Elizabeth Stock was born 18 February 1855
(GRO certificate): on 25 January 1869 she was thirteen. Either the ages are overstated by two and four years
(the 1871 census overstated hers by two), or this is another Eliza Stock, born about 1852-54.
PLACE: Great Dunmow, in north-west Essex, is in the same part of the county as Wethersfield (distance not checked
here: general knowledge, to verify before any use).
IDENTITY NOT SETTLED. Remains on the DO NOT USE list (live_lists 5). Decisive checks: (1) GRO birth index, Dunmow and
Braintree districts, 1850-1856, for any other Eliza or Elizabeth Stock; (2) the 1861 census for an Eliza Stock aged
about 7-10 in that area other than Elizabeth (at Wethersfield, age 6); (3) the HO 140/5 page itself (degree of
instruction, previous convictions); (4) any second report naming her parish.
