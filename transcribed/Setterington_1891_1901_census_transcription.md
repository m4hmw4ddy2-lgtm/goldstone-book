## Notes (Claude, 14 October 2026)
- George Setterington was a COLOUR SERGEANT of the 31st (East Surrey Regiment) in April 1891, living in the barracks
  at Kingston; by 1901 he was a skilled labourer in Chatham. The 1910 marriage register gives him as "George William
  Setterington, deceased", "Labourer".
- Clarie Irene: born at Kingston (both censuses); aged 1 in 1891 and 12 in 1901, which fits 7 June 1889. In 1901 she
  is "Irene", living in CHATHAM, Luton ward. So she grew up in Chatham before her marriage.
- ELMA ROSE: both enumerators write "Elma" (1891 "Elma Rose"; 1901 "Elma"), and her BIRTH WAS REGISTERED as "Elma Rose
  Setterington" (GRO birth index, Jan-Feb-Mar quarter 1891, Kingston, vol. 2a, p. 327; Rik, 14 October 2026). Elma is
  her registered name. "Alma Rose" is the family's form and the form on the 1921 census (Alma Rose Setterington, in
  Albert's household) and in Lily Alma Rose Goldstone's names. (Rik first read "Elma" as an index error, then
  confirmed it from the GRO index the same day.)
- Ages do not run evenly: Priscilla 28 (1891) and 44 (1901); "Elma" 4 months and 9. Not to be used for dating.
- Priscilla's birthplace: Shorncliffe (1891) against a faint reading like Sheerness (1901): unsettled.
- The mother is Priscilla in both: the oral history's "possibly Mary" (dramatis_personae.md) is not supported.
