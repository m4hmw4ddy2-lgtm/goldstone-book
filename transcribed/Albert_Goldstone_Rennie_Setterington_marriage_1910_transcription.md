# Marriage of Albert Goldstone and Rennie Setterington, 5 March 1910: transcription

**Image:** sources/images/marriage-certificates/Marriage Cert Albert Goldstone - Rene Setterington 1910.jpeg
**Document:** GRO certified copy, application 856272-3, MXE 134106, given 20 January 2009. Printed certificate:
"CERTIFIED to be a true copy of an entry in the certified copy of a register of Marriages in the Registration
District of Medway". So the handwriting is the CLERGY'S QUARTERLY COPY sent to the registrar, not the church
register: the "signatures" are copied names, NOT Albert's or Rennie's own hands. Nothing in prose is to call them
his or her signature.

cells enlarged two to three times. Readings Claude left open are marked [?]; Rik settled col. 5 ("Able Seaman") and the curate ("Hetherington") by eye the same day. The first witness, "Thomas Knowles", was also
checked by eye by Rik the same day.

## The officiating clergyman (added 13 October 2026)
Crockford's Clerical Directory, 1932 edition, entry supplied by Rik as a clip (edition given by Rik, 13 October
2026): sources/images/marriage-certificates/Crockford 1932 Hetherington William.png. Transcribed from the clip:
"HETHERINGTON, William.—Hatf. Hall, Dur. L.Th. 1893. d 1894, p 1896 Southw. C. of St. Paul, Nottm. 1894-98;
Tideswell, Buxton, 1898-1903; Snodland, Kent, 1905-06; St. Mk. New Brompton, 1906-16; V. of W. Butterwick, Dio. Linc.
from 1916. (P, V. of Owston; Gross Inc. 352l, Net 340l and Ho; Pop. 656.) West Butterwick Vicarage, Scunthorpe, Lincs."
So in March 1910 he was curate of St Mark's, New Brompton (1906-16), the church where the marriage was taken. The
identification rests on name, title and place agreeing; the clip does not name the marriage.
(Samuel Denne, The History and Antiquities of Rochester, 2nd ed., 1817, was suggested and not opened: published
before St Mark's existed, it cannot name him.)
