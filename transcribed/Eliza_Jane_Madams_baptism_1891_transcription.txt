READINGS NOTED:
- BOTH DAUGHTERS WERE BAPTISED THE SAME DAY, Saturday 17 October 1891 (Nos. 832 and 833): Lilian at eighteen months,
  Eliza Jane at a month. Twelve days later (Thursday 29 October) Elizabeth registered Eliza Jane's birth as "E. Madams,
  Mother", describing herself as "Elizabeth Madams late Goldstone formerly Stock".
- The birth date, 16 September 1891, agrees with the certificate.
