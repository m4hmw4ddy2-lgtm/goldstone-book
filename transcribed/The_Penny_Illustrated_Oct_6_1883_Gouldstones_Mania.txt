TRANSCRIPTION

GOULDSTONE’S MANIA.

After the official announcement which was made on Monday, there can be no doubt that Gouldstone, the Walthamstow murderer, will be removed to the Criminal Lunatic Asylum. Dr. Orange, the Medical Superintendent of the Broadmoor Asylum, who was sent down to Chelmsford to see Gouldstone, entertains no doubt of his insanity. His mother and aunt were insane, and his great-uncle committed suicide.
