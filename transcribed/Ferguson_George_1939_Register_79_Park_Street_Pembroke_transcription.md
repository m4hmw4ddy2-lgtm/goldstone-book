## Findings (notes only)

1. GEORGE'S DATE OF BIRTH: "24 May" "20". This SOURCES the day previously unsourced in dramatis_personae.md. It fits the 1921 census closely enough (1 year 1 month on 19 June 1921 would want mid-April to mid-May; a few days' slip).
2. IN SEPTEMBER 1939 GEORGE WAS IN PEMBROKE, PEMBROKESHIRE, and Vera was in Bath. The Register does not show them meeting; it shows they were not in the same town at the outbreak of war. They gave the same address, 35 Dagmar Road, at their marriage in March 1942.
3. THE HOUSEHOLD is a working party: twelve men on the page entered as aircraft riveters, aircraft fitters or steel benders (rows 6 to 17, George included; row 11's name struck through in red, so "at least eleven" if that line is a deletion), six of them born in 1920 (rows 6, 7, 10, 11, 12, 13) and none in 1919 or 1921 [CORRECTED 30 Oct 2026 from "at least eleven ... eight of them born in 1920 or within a year of it", a miscount; recounted from the table above], lodging with a crane driver's family. That they were sent there by one employer is INFERENCE. Which employer is not in the record. LEAD (not verified): Short Brothers of Rochester, makers of the Sunderland, ran a flying-boat repair organisation, and Pembroke Dock was an RAF flying-boat station. See notes/live_lists.md, section 4, item 97.
4. OCCUPATION: "aircraft Rivetor" in 1939; "aircraft fitter" at his marriage (1942) and Michael's birth (1943).
