Under the heading "ENGAGEMENTS":
"NEAVES—GOLDSTONE.—The engagement is announced between Charles George, eldest son of Mr. and the late Mrs. Neaves,
of 71, Canterbury Road, Sittingbourne, and Doris May, youngest daughter of Mr. and Mrs. A. Goldstone, of 35, Dagmar Road,
Chatham."
(The next notice, WALLING-READ, of Eccles and Hartlip, is unrelated.)
NOTES (not prose):
1. **ALBERT AND CLARIE AT 35 DAGMAR ROAD.** "Mr. and Mrs. A. Goldstone, of 35, Dagmar Road" with "Doris May, youngest
   daughter" identifies them beyond reasonable doubt. So, at the date of the notice (before the Q3 1949 marriage),
   35 Dagmar Road was ALBERT AND CLARIE'S address: the house where Vera and George both lived at their marriage (1942)
   and at Michael's birth (1943), and where Vera lived until 1979. It fills the gap after 33A Connaught Road (1939).
   It bears on Rik's testimony that Dagmar Road was "Vera and George's house" (28 Oct): both may be true at different
   times (the Goldstones' house, which Vera and George shared and later had), or the households overlapped. For Rik;
   Albert's death certificate (1962, item 60) and the electoral registers would show who lived there when.
2. "youngest daughter": Doris was the youngest (born 1928); Brian, the youngest child, had died in 1937.
3. Press as press: the paper named once the clip's source is known; the notice's facts are of record kind (names,
   relationships, addresses) and may be stated.
