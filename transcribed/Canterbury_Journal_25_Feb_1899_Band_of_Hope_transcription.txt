Text, verbatim:
"BAPTIST CHAPEL BAND OF HOPE.—The winter treat to the members of the above was held in the schoolroom on Tuesday
evening last, when about fifty children and friends were present. Recitations were given by some of the members,
while the sleight of hand illustrations shown by Mr. Fred Porter were fully appreciated by the juveniles. A vote of
thanks was passed to Mr. Porter for his kindness, on the proposition of Richard Taylor, seconded by Jane Philpot, a
similar compliment being paid to Miss Walker for collecting the necessary funds for the plentiful supply of sweets,
nuts, and oranges which were provided. The latter resolution was proposed by Albert Madams aad seconded by Caroline
Blackman."

DATE: "Tuesday evening last" before Saturday 25 February 1899 = Tuesday 21 February 1899 (calendar computation).
Albert was eleven.

PLACE NOT ON THE CLIP: the town heading above the item is cut off. The Canterbury Journal covered several towns.
