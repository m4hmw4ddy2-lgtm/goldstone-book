TRANSCRIPTION

The man Wm. Gouldstone, who is charged with murdering his five children at his residence, 8, Courtenay-place,
Walthamstow, on the 8th inst., was again brought up before the magistrates at Stratford on Saturday. The evidence of
Mrs. Ada Hamilton, who acted as nurse to the wife of the accused, was recapitulated, and that of the constables and the
doctor who had charge of the case added, after which the prisoner was committed for trial.
