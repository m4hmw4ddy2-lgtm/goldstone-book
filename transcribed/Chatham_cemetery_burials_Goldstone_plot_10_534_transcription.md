READINGS AND FINDINGS (notes, not prose):
1. The three are in ONE grave: 10 / 534. Find a Grave (memorials 224968936-38) agrees; this list is the better witness.
2. The dates are BURIAL dates (Brian: died 15 May 1937 by his certificate, buried 20 May). So Find a Grave's "death"
   dates are burial dates throughout, as suspected: Clarie was buried on 7 January 1958 (her death a few days before:
   possibly late December 1957, the index giving only registration in Jan-Mar 1958); Albert was buried on 26 June 1962.
3. Albert's age at burial, 76, with burial on 26 June: by the naval 19 June 1886 he was 76 from 19 June 1962, so if the
   age carries the false year, he died between 19 June and a few days before the 26th. His true age was 75. The
   certificate (on order) decides the day.
4. Albert died at ALL SAINTS HOSPITAL, CHATHAM: the hospital named on Rik's own birth certificate (31 May 1970). A fact
   for Part Six, in Rik's voice, if anywhere; no juxtaposition in Part Five's narrator.
5. "Retired": his description in 1962.
6. Brian: "s/o Albert Goldstone (Shipwright)": SHIPWRIGHT, against "Rigger H.M.D." on Brian's death certificate (1937)
   and "ship rigger" everywhere else (1930, 1933, 1939, 1942). A new contradiction (proposed 127): register's (or the
   transcriber's) word against the certificate. The certificate governs for the prose.
7. ABBREVIATIONS, from the KFHS "LIST OF ABBREVIATIONS" (uploaded by Rik 22 October 2026; read the same day; the list
   itself warns that it "is not a comprehensive list" and that some meanings "have been lost"):
   "10', 12' etc" = "The grave was dug to a depth of 10 feet, 12 feet etc"; "2nd Inter, 3rd Inter etc" = "The second,
   third etc burial in that grave"; "C" = "Consecrated ground"; "P" = "Purchased or possibly parish, pauper or public";
   "s/o" = "Son of". So: the grave was dug ten feet for Brian in 1937 (room for later burials: inference); Clarie's
   (1958, 8') was the second burial in it; Albert's (1962, 6') is ALSO entered "2nd Inter.", though by the order of
   burials it was the third: a slip in the register or the transcription (not resolved; the council's register would
   settle it). Albert's "P": purchased, or parish, pauper or public (the list cannot say which). All in consecrated
   ground. None of this for prose beyond the shared grave.
8. Parish column "Christ Church" for all three: the ecclesiastical parish (or ward) of the home, Christ Church, Luton
   (Albert's 1934 ward). Brian's adds the address, 33a Connaught Road.
9. The cemetery's NAME: KFHS calls it "Chatham Cemetery" (Maidstone Road and Palmerston Road); Find a Grave "Chatham
   Palmerston Road Cemetery". RULED (Rik, 22 October 2026): the prose calls it Palmerston Road Cemetery.

ADDED 22 October 2026 (Rik, from the KFHS 1869-2007 "by grave" file, which he holds locally): the by-grave listing gives
the same information for 10 / 534: the three burials only, in the one plot, at the depths above. Rik's reading of the
"P" on Albert's entry: purchased ("Albert paid"). The abbreviations list gives "Purchased or possibly parish, pauper or
public"; the transcript does not say who bought the grave or when. For prose: the shared grave may be stated; who paid
is Rik's reading, to be stated only in a form the entry supports (e.g. "a purchased grave"), not "Albert paid for it",
unless the council's register names the purchaser.
