The page has no heading naming an institution; the column is headed "(1) RELATION to Head of Family—or (2) Position
in the Institution", and the page lists unrelated people, many born at Great Sampford, with some family groups. The
place, "Saffron Walden Union Workhouse, Sewers End Road", comes from Findmypast's index (transcript
GBC/1871/0010471337), NOT from this page.
CONFIRMED 20 October 2026 from the enumeration book's cover (sources/images/census-stuff/ESSRG10_1709_1711-0161.jpg,
RG10/1709, folio 99, checked in full): "ENUMERATION BOOK For the undermentioned Institution or Establishment. Name and
Description Saffron Walden Union Workhouse. Where Situate Sewers End Road, Saffron Walden", Superintendent Registrar's
District and Sub-District Saffron Walden. Joseph's page (folio 104, page 7) is five folios on in the same piece and
uses the institution form ("Position in the Institution"), so it belongs to this book. (The intervening folios were
not seen; a second institution starting between them is very unlikely but not excluded.)
