TRANSCRIPTION

[We understand that D]r. Orange, the medical superintendent of Broadmoor, who was recently sent down to
Chelmsford to see the convict Gouldstone, entertains no doubt of his insanity. His mother and aunt were both
insane, and his great uncle committed suicide. He himself appears to have had a suicidal mania, and it is
believed that he went out with the intention of drowning himself on the very day that he murdered his children.
His wife, who is a very respectable young woman, says that he has shown signs of madness for the last 12 months.
