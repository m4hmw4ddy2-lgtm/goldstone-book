NOTE: reports the commutation to penal servitude for life "on the ground of insanity" as done, on the same day as the
Glasgow Herald's "It is understood" and a day before the BMJ's "We are informed". It never happened (respite made
indefinite 1 October; certified 10 October; Broadmoor 15 October). Press only. Its own point, new to the record:
those who think hanging the milder punishment ask whether a life sentence could justly outlast the time he ought
to spend in an asylum, "if he is insane at all". "insance" is the page's misprint for "insane".

TRANSCRIPTION

Gouldstone's sentence has been commuted to penal servitude for life on the ground of insanity, and now those who hold
that hanging is a milder punishment of the two want to know whether the sentence can justly be enforced beyond the
period in which Gouldstone, if he is insance at all, should be confined in a lunatic asylum.
