NOTES
- "U D" is written after both Rochester and Chatham as it stands in the image; read as written, not expanded.
- "H.M.D.": as written. Its expansion (His Majesty's Dockyard) is not given on the certificate; Chatham Dockyard is
  where Albert worked after the Navy (dramatis_personae.md), but the expansion is ours and not to be quoted as the record's.
- The doctor's initial is a single letter followed by a stroke, before "O'Brien": P or R, not settled.
- "(a)": the bracketed letter is small and could be read as (o); "1 (a)" is the printed-form convention for the
  immediate cause, and is the reading taken.
- 33a Connaught Road is the address on the 1939 Register (dramatis_personae.md).
