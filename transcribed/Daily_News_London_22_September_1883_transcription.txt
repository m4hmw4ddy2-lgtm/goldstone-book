TRANSCRIPTION

THE WALTHAMSTOW MURDERS.—The Central News states that the Home Secretary is about to cause a medical inquiry to be
held into the mental condition of William Gouldstone, who was sentenced to death for the murder of his five children at
Walthamstow on the 8th August.

TRANSCRIBER'S NOTES

1. An agency (Central News) paragraph: likely printed widely the same day. One witness, however many papers ran it.
2. Dates the public announcement of the inquiry to Friday 21 or Saturday 22 September: eight days after the sentence,
   four days before the respite of 26 September. The BMJ of 29 September, on commutation, follows.
