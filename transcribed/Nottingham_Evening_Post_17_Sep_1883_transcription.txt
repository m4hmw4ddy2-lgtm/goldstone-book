NOTES:
1. DATED MONDAY 17 SEPTEMBER, NINE DAYS BEFORE THE RESPITE. "If executed at all" is the paper's own doubt on the
   Monday after the trial, the day the workmen's and juror's letters and Savage's appeared. It belongs to CHAPTER
   SIXTEEN (approved 9 October), not Seventeen.
2. New: the applications for Marwood's post went to the Home Secretary, and Harcourt sent the whole pile to the City
   authorities, "it was necessary to remove them from Whitehall in a cab"; the choice nominally the Sheriff of
   Essex's, in practice the Sheriffs of London and Middlesex's. Chapter Sixteen says men had written "to the gaol and
   to the High Sheriff" (Essex Weekly News, 21 Sep): compatible, not a contradiction.
3. "A meeting of the General Purposes Committee of the Court of Aldermen was held yesterday": in a Monday evening
   paper, Sunday 16 September, unless the paragraph is copied from a paper of another day (likely; an agency or
   London item). Not to be dated from it.
4. "at at all": the page's doubled word across a line break. Press only.

TRANSCRIPTION

The whole of the applications made to the Home Secretary for the post of hangman, have been forwarded by order of
Sir William Harcourt, to the authorities of the city of London, who will take the initiative in appointing the
executioner in successor to Marwood. As Gouldstone, the Walthamstow murderer, will be executed in Essex, if executed
at at all, the selection of hangman will nominally rest with the sheriff of that county; but the sheriffs of London
and Middlesex have been consulted, and the person appointed will, in the first instance, as a matter of fact, be
nominated by them, and this is the reason why the applications received at the Home Office have been sent to the
city. They form an immense pile, and it was necessary to remove them from Whitehall in a cab. A meeting of the
General Purposes Committee of the Court of Aldermen was held yesterday preparatory to steps being taken in the
matter, and it is expected that the name of the new functionary will be known in a few days.
