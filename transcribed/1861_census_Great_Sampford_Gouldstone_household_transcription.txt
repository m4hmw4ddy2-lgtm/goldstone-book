NOTES:
- The July note (MASTER_NOTES, "immediate 1861 neighbours": Norris, Ruffle, Watson, Drane, with the Gouldstones as
  "Family 10") is CONTRADICTED by this page: the smithy is schedule 5, after Philpott, Porter, Martin and Gray. The
  Chapter Two draft's sentence built on the July note is replaced (21 October 2026).
- William Philpott: the "W. Phillpott" with whom Thomas occupied the Orchard (Lot 32, 1867) is very probably he; the
  prose does not say so. George Gray: the "George Grey" of Lot 27 (1867) and probably the carpenter's son "named Gray"
  taken ill in the cholera (1866). Notes only.
