GREAT BARDFIELD, NOV. 30.
Magistrates present—Col. Brise, M.P., in the chair; and the Rev. W. E. L. Lampet.
...
The licence of the Three Horseshoes beerhouse, Great Bardfield, was transferred from Charles Dowsett to Mr. James
Gouldstone, blacksmith.

NOTES:
- Sessions of Monday 30 November 1874. James is Thomas's brother (1861 Little Sampford and 1871 Great Bardfield
  households match: PART_ONE_search_list.md, item 10).
- Also at these sessions: "Edward Stock, labourer, of Finchingfield", fined for assaulting a police sergeant over his
  boy's armful of barley straw (5 November). A Stock of Finchingfield, where Thomas Stock was baptised; no link drawn.
- The Great Bardfield bench also covered Great Sampford (the 1866 drainage summons): Thomas Gouldstone's own beer
  licence for the White House would have passed through it, about 1870-71. Not yet found.
