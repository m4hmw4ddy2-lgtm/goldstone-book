TRANSCRIPTION

THE CONVICT GOULDSTONE.

The Home Secretary yesterday addressed a communication to the Sheriff of Essex, signifying the Queen's command that
the execution of the sentence of death passed upon William Gouldstone, the Walthamstow murderer, be respited until
further signification of Her Majesty's pleasure. This extended respite is in consequence of the report made to
Sir William Harcourt by Dr. Clarke and Dr. Orange, who last week had an interview with the convict to ascertain
the condition of his mind.
