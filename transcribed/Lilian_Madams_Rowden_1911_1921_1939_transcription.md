# LILIAN FLORENCE ISABEL MADAMS (MRS ROWDEN), ALBERT'S HALF-SISTER: 1911 CENSUS, 1914 MARRIAGE INDEX, 1921 CENSUS, 1939 REGISTER
Created 29 October 2026 (Part Six; live_lists 4, item 95). Images uploaded by Rik the same day to sources/images/census-stuff/.
Piece references are not on the images (to record from the site if wanted).

## 1. 1911 CENSUS, WHITSTABLE (image: "Lily Madams census 1911.jpg", 6376 x 3744)
IMAGE CHECK (check-once rule): 29 October 2026 (Claude): whole schedule read; rows 1-3 and the foot cropped and enlarged.
Schedule "163". Ten rooms ("10 rooms - private house"). Postal address "R[o?]se Cottage Whitstable" (first word not certain;
"Rose" likeliest). Signed "Marie Louisa Peall" [surname not certain: Peall?].
- 1 "Marie Louisa Peall" [?], 68, single, "Private Means", born London.
- 2 "Mary Anna Peall" [?], "Sister", 66, single, "Private Means", born London.
- 3 "Lily Madams", age "2[1 or 2]" (the second figure unclear), single, "Domestic Servant General" ("Domestic Servant"
  first written, struck, rewritten), born "Whitstable".
NOTES: Lilian, born 7 April 1890, was 20 on census night (2 April 1911): the age given is one or two years over. A general
servant to two elderly sisters of private means, five years after her mother's death. NOT at Albert's (he was aboard
Dominion; his wife at 46 Edinburgh Road, Chatham).

## 2. MARRIAGE, GRO INDEX (Rik's paste, 29 October 2026; index text only)
"Lilian F I Madams", spouse surname "Rowden", registered Jul-Aug-Sep 1914, Blean district, Kent, vol. 2a, p. 2166. The
search site's "Household members" list beside it (George A Allen, Lilian F I Madams, William J Rowden, Florence Smith) is
the four names on that index page, not a household. (Ancestry tree: married 13 August 1914, citing his WWI pension papers,
WO 364, piece 4886B: NOT seen.)

## 3. 1921 CENSUS, WHITSTABLE (image: "Lillian Rowden census 1921.jpg", 6456 x 3033)
IMAGE CHECK (check-once rule): 29 October 2026 (Claude): whole schedule read; rows 1-2 cropped and enlarged.
Schedule "270" (overwritten). Address not on the page (no cover). Four rooms. Signed "W J Rowden".
- 1 "William John Rowden", Head, 33 years 5 months, married, born Whitstable, Kent; "Hair Dresser", "Own account", place of
  work "At Home".
- 2 "Lilian F I Rowden", Wife, 31 years 10 months, married, born Whitstable, Kent; "Home Duties".
- 3 "William C. Rowden", Son, 5 years 7 months, born Whitstable; school "Whole time".
- 4 "John Rowden", Son, 4 years 5 months, born Whitstable; school "Whole time".
- 5 "Madge Rowden", daughter, 1 year 10 months, born Whitstable.
Column (n), living children under 16: "3". Enumerator's totals: 3 males, 2 females, 5 persons, 4 rooms.
NOTES: her age, 31y10m on 19 June 1921, is eight months over (born 7 April 1890: 31y2m). His trade, hairdresser on his own
account, working at home: the "barber" of Dot's memory (via Steve, 2015).

## 4. 1939 REGISTER, WHITSTABLE (image: "Lillian Rowden 1939 Register.jpg", 3709 x 4507)
IMAGE CHECK (check-once rule): 29 October 2026 (Claude): the page read; schedule 309 cropped and enlarged.
Header: E.D. letter code "DIZB"; "Whitstable" U.D.; registration district and sub-district "59/2". Three lines on the page
officially closed.
Address "97" (the street from the column above; the MyHeritage index text gives "97, High Street, Whitstable"), schedule 309:
- 1 Rowden, William J., M, 13/2/88, M[arried], "Hairdresser (Proprietor)".
- 2 Rowden, Lilian F. I., F, 7/4/90, M, "unpaid domestic duty" (as written: "unpaid Domestic Duty").
- 3 Rowden, Madge, F, 4/9/[19], S, "Cinema Attendant"; a later annotation over her line, "D[?]B (23/7/45)" [reading not
  certain; very probably a change-of-name or marriage note].
NOTES: Lilian's birth date, 7 April, is her certificate's (7 April 1890): the line is certainly her. In 1939 the Rowdens kept
a hairdresser's at 97 High Street; her brother Albert was at 33A Connaught Road, Chatham.

## WHAT THIS SETTLES (for the notes; nothing for prose yet)
Dot's memory "'Auntie Lil' was married to a barber" (Steve's email, 22 April 2015) is confirmed by the record: Lilian Madams
married William John Rowden, hairdresser, in 1914, and was his wife in 1921 and 1939. (Dot's "The property the Madams lived in
in Harbour Street, Whitstable, was used as a hairdressers" is NOT confirmed: the Rowdens' 1939 address is the High Street.)
## 5. DEATH, GRO INDEX (Rik's paste, 29 October 2026; index text only)
"Lilian Florence I Rowden", death age 90, birth date 7 Apr 1890, registered Jan-Feb-Mar 1981, Canterbury district, Kent,
vol. 16, p. 0256. The birth date (given in the index from 1969) is her certificate's: certainly her. Died aged 90, early 1981
(the day not in the index). Canterbury registration district (Whitstable lay in it by then: the trees give Whitstable).
