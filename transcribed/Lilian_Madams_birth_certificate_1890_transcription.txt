READINGS NOTED:
- Weekdays (calendar computation): born Monday 7 April 1890; registered Monday 19 May 1890.
- BEACH ALLEY: Lilian was born there, and Elizabeth gave it as her own residence, in April-May 1890. She died there
  on 29 June 1906 (death certificate). The first and last addresses of hers in Whitstable are the same alley.
- ELIZABETH IS THE INFORMANT, and gives the same description of herself as on Eliza Jane's certificate seventeen
  months later: "Elizabeth Madams late Goldstone formerly Stock". The October 1891 registration is therefore the
  SECOND such document, not the only one (PART_FOUR_plan.md finding 1 and the Chapter Nineteen skeleton to be
  corrected).
- This is now the first document of Elizabeth in Whitstable (before: the census of 5 April 1891).
- Same registrar (Edwin Johnson) as in 1891 and 1906.
