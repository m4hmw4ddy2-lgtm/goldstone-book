WHAT IT GIVES: Albert Goldstone, Labour, lost Christ Church ward to the sitting Conservative, Frances M. Freeman,
by 505 votes to 832 (38 per cent of the votes cast; 1,337 of 3,545 electors voted). With the Observer of 19 October
1934 ("hitherto been unsuccessful in his attempts"), he had now stood at least twice and lost every time held.
