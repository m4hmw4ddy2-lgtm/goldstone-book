## FINDINGS
1. **Vera's own documents are few.** After Brian: the Civil Service pass (June 1937), the school certificate (July
   1937), the 1939 Register (Bath), the marriage (1942), Michael's birth as informant (1943). After 1943 nothing of hers
   is held until 1979. The chapter is "through Vera" by its frame (it starts and, in 1962, ends with the two men she
   lost), but its middle is the record of Albert, Clarie and the club. That is what the documents hold; the chapter
   should not try to make her more present than they do (Ch24 does that, in Rik's voice).
2. **The 1939 Register writes the change of name onto the page.** Vera's line: "Goldstone" struck through, "FERGUSON"
   written over it; in the margin "Cl.20 M [6 or 8]/5/42" (an annotation of her marriage, dated May 1942, two months
   after it). A plain document fact. It belongs to 1942, by the linear rule (the annotation was made then), and could
   stand beside the marriage. Placing it is close to pointing; F.
3. **The Bath question.** Why Vera was in Bath, and in which department, is NOT on the page. (General knowledge that
   Admiralty departments moved to Bath in 1939 is TO CHECK and, even if true, says nothing of her.) Out unless a
   document or Rik's family supplies it (questions below).
4. **35 Dagmar Road** is the Part's house: Vera and George both of it at the marriage (1942) and at Michael's birth
   (1943); Vera of it at her death (1979). Whose house it was in 1942 is NOT shown by any document; Rik's testimony (28
   Oct, below): Vera and George's. Ch23 gives the address plainly; what it was to the family is Ch24's.
5. **42 Magpie Hall Road.** Rik (handover; PART_SIX_plan): the old Medway Union Workhouse site, and where Michael, Rob
   and Rik's mother were born. SOURCE (Rik, 28 Oct): Medway Council, "Corridors of care: All Saints Hospital"
   (medway.gov.uk/info/200487/health_matters_corridors_of_care/1332/all_saints_hospital; read 28 Oct by WebFetch, a
   summary tool, so its sentences are to be re-read on the page before quoting): the hospital "was housed in some of
   the original workhouse buildings on Magpie Hall Road"; it "used to be known as The Medway Hospital or The County
   Hospital"; named All Saints' on joining the NHS in 1948; later "the main maternity hospital for the area"; services
   moved to Medway Maritime Hospital by 1999. NOT on the page: the number 42, or that the hospital stood there in 1943
   under another name. So in 1943 the certificate's "42 Magpie Hall Road" is very probably the hospital (then not yet
   "All Saints"), but the number needs a second source (a directory, or a birth certificate of the 1940s naming the
   hospital at 42). And it was not "All Saints" in 1943: any sentence must keep the names to their years.
   Ch23 gives the address only. Its identity with All Saints, and any echo of Albert's birth in a workhouse infirmary,
   is for Rik's voice in Ch24, after a source is saved (and the echo, if made at all, made by Rik as his own).
