**Ada Hamilton — Witness Statement**
`sources/trial-docs/Witness statements/Ada Hamilton Nurse/Ada_Hamilton_Witness_Statement_1883.txt`
Status: READ
Key content: Full account of the evening of 8 August 1883. Ada washing twins in bedroom. Frederick on the bed in the bedroom (not in the kitchen — he was taken there by William). William coming home. The cord in William's hands. The cistern. The perambulator moved. The three boys in a row. The locked door. Elizabeth screaming. William with the hammer. NOTE: this witness statement (sworn 9 Aug, resworn 11 Aug) says WILLIAM took Frederick to the kitchen — the coroner deposition (10 Aug) says Elizabeth took him. ### 1.2 Coroner's Depositions
*Location: sources/trial-docs/Coroner Depositions/*
*All fully READ*

**Ada Hamilton — Coroner Deposition**
`sources/trial-docs/Coroner Depositions/Ada Hamilton/Ada Hamilton.txt`
Status: READ
Key content: EARLIEST SWORN DOCUMENT (10 August 1883). Several unique details not in other sources: William "only smiled but made no reply" when Ada spoke to him on arrival — this is the only source for the smile. "William slept in the kitchen" during Elizabeth's confinement. Gouldstones "occupied a back room, kitchen and scullery on the first floor" — most precise building description in the corpus. Hamiltons "occupied the front room on the second floor" — but all later testimony says same floor; probably loose use of "second floor" to mean first floor above ground. William "said it was a very large family, and he wished they were dead" — Ada thought he meant the twins. NOTE: deposition says Elizabeth (not William) took Frederick to the kitchen — contradicts all other sources; see master notes Contradictions section.
Book section: Prologue, Part Two

**Emma Clarke — Coroner Deposition**
`sources/trial-docs/Coroner Depositions/Emma Clarke/Emma Clarke.txt`
Status: READ
Key content: Confirms husband's name is CHARLES (not James as in all press accounts). Address given as "No. 1 Courtnay Place" — confirmed transcription error; correct address is No. 8. William's words when he opened the door, confirmed under cross-examination: "You can come in, it is all done." Clarke heard Elizabeth say "He has killed my babies." Clarke heard Elizabeth say she wished children dead — "when she was worried, not in earnest."
Book section: Part Two

`sources/trial-docs/Trial Account Old Bailey website.txt`
