## FINDINGS FOR THIS CHAPTER
1. The particulars the Prologue did not use: "Labourer" struck through, "Farm Labourer" beneath (and "[G1?]"). He
   had been a basket boy and a harbour labourer at Whitstable (Chapter Nineteen). Neither the date of the change nor
   its hand is recorded.
2. The engagement date is his birthday by the sheet. Arithmetic: his eighteenth by 1886, his seventeenth by his birth
   certificate. Ordinary Seaman the same day.
3. The cells: three spells; the sheet gives no offence and no date (plan, finding 2, holds the inference: NOT for
   prose). The conduct class twice lowered to second, twice restored.
4. [REVISED, plan DECIDE 6 (d)] Her death: the Jupiter entry is given by its own dates, 12 March 1906 to 4 August
   1907, and nothing is said of her there. A service sheet records postings, not leave; "unbroken" is not to be used.
   Rik: "We will find a way to include a single line callback": to be found; it may not claim more than the sheet holds.
5. The marriage: he was in Pembroke I from 1 March; married on the 5th. His age "22" (true). "Rennie" Setterington, 22
   (true 20); both fathers "deceased"; his rank "Able Seaman"; both of New Brompton; witnesses Thomas Knowles and
   Ethel Cooper (Knowles visually checked by Rik, 13 October 2026). NO TRANSCRIPTION OF THE REGISTER EXISTS IN THE
   REPO: one to be made and checked word by word against the image before drafting (check-once rule).
6. [REVISED, new] The false year runs on past this chapter: the 1911 census aboard Dominion gives 24 (the naval 1886).
   So far the 1910 register is the only document after 1887 to give his true age. Its "22" stands with no comment
   (H).
7. [REVISED, new, plan DECIDE 8 amended] Albert is to be given life: where the sheet has him on a ship across a date,
   that ship's documented doings may be told as his. For this chapter's ships (Lion, Agincourt, Prince George,
   Majestic, Firequeen, Victory, Jupiter, Pembroke, Shannon) the histories are NOT YET SOURCED; each needs a saved
   source before it is offered for selection (I).

## DECIDE POINTS (beyond the plan's, which govern)
**A. The occupation line, how given.** (a) **As the sheet shows it: "Labourer", struck through, and "Farm Labourer"
written beneath, without who or when** (recommended). (b) With the Whitstable trades set beside it in a clause
("basket boy", "harbour labourer"). Against: Chapter Nineteen has them; the reader can join them.

**B. The engagement and his age.** (a) **The date and the twelve years only; the reader holds 1887 and the sheet's
1886** (recommended). Nothing on why the engagement began on that day: that a boy's twelve years ran from
his eighteenth birthday is general knowledge, not yet sourced (CHAPTER_19_skeleton.md, 13 October; plan, finding 19). (b) One clause: his seventeenth birthday, though the sheet made it his eighteenth.

**C. The collision, October 1903.** [REVISED, plan DECIDE 8 amended] (a) **In, from a contemporary report saved to the

without placing him. Against: Rik's ruling on 8. (c) Out.

them; the conduct class lowered and restored, twice; no dates, no offence** (recommended). (b) A single sentence:

own summary of those years, and it sits beside the cells without comment). (b) Out.
