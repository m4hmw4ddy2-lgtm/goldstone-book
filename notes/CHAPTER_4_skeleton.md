## SPAN AND QUESTION
Autumn 1878 to 15 March 1879. William leaves the smithy world for a London ironworks and a new trade; Elizabeth, alone
since 1873, is found in the same house; they marry.

## SOURCES
- William Graves, sworn, Old Bailey, 14 September 1883 (sources/trial-docs/Trial Account Old Bailey website.txt):
  foreman of the department at the Falkirk Ironworks, Upper Thames Street; "known the prisoner ever since he was born";
  "came to the works about five years ago, and married some four or five months afterwards"; cross-examined: "When he
  first came to work for this firm he was a blacksmith; we then made him a whitesmith". Used as FACTS, no trial
  words (the Emily precedent, Ch2 B): "had known him since the day he was born" is a paraphrase of the fact.
- Graves's life: baptism 25 January 1829, Radwinter; 1851 (22, lodger, Tower Hamlets, whitesmith); 1861 (32, Bethnal
  Green, wife Harriet, five children); 1871 (4 Mare Street, Hackney, wife Martha, chandler shop keeper);
  1881 and 1891, 88 Frampton Park Road (dramatis_personae.md; MASTER_NOTES). "Next parish to Great Sampford":
  Radwinter borders Great Sampford (old Ch5; TO CONFIRM on a map before final).
- London City Press, 30 October 1878, p. 3 (checked 20 October; transcribed): Falkirk Iron Company, 67 Upper Thames
  Street, Honourable Mention, Class 27 (the class not named in the prose: what it covered is not stated).
- Blacksmith and whitesmith: Isaac Taylor (1836), as recorded in MASTER_NOTES ("large and coarse articles, as
  horse-shoes, ploughshares, chains"; "articles of neater and more delicate form, as locks, keys, carpenters'
  tools"). Paraphrased; general knowledge from a period source, recorded here.
- Marriage certificate, St Luke's, Hackney, 15 March 1879, no. 248 (sources/images/marriage-certificates/William
  Gouldstone-Elizabeth Stock Marriage.jpg; viewed 9 September and re-read 21 October 2026): William Goldstone, 22,
  bachelor, Iron Worker, 88 Frampton Pk Rd, father Thomas Goldstone, Smith; Elizabeth Stock, 24, spinster, [dash],
  88 Frampton Pk Rd, father Thomas Stock, "(dead)"; after banns; John C. Harding; witnesses Thomas Goldstone, his mark,
  and Emily Goldstone. William signed "William Goldstone". 15 March 1879 was a Saturday (computed).

## FINDINGS
- The certificate gives William's occupation as "Iron Worker", not whitesmith or smith.
- The surname is "Goldstone" throughout the entry, in William's signature and in Emily's. The form the family line
  later uses (Albert, Vera) appears here first in William's own hand.
