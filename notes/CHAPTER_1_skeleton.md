## SHAPE (three movements)

1. **The certificate.** Open, as ruled, on Elizabeth's birth and its registration. NEW dated moment: Thomas
   registering her on the thirtieth of March, six weeks after the birth, and making his mark. Her mother's name "went
   down in full", and the three names lead straight into who Jemima had been.
2. **What the names carried.** Jemima's first family, told forward from 1833 as background (the one step back the
   chapter takes, allowed before the eighth): the wedding, the seven children, the six deaths, the 1851 household, the
   second wedding with Smee at the register again. Largely the present Chapter Two's run, which is strong; tightened
   under G, and Edward Suckling cut to a clause (DECIDE B).
3. **Two births.** Back to 1855 and forward: Nathan's death in July 1856, with Elizabeth seventeen months old. Then,
   a few miles north, William's birth in October 1856 and Emily registering it herself (the forge, the mill, the Cock
   Inn; "at least the fourth generation" in one sentence, under E). The chapter ends on George's burial in July 1858:
   of Jemima's eight, only Emma left. That hands to Chapter Two, which opens on census night 1861 with Emma at home.
