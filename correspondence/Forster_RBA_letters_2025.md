## WHAT THE SECOND LETTER CHANGES — three corrections, 22 September 2026

**1. The access route is not closed. It is unlikely, and the archive has already said so.**
MASTER_NOTES was written earlier this evening to say that nobody can satisfy the descent test and that
"the silence that protects him is absolute". The first half stands: William left no descendants, and Rik
descends from Elizabeth, not from him. The second half is now too strong as a statement about ACCESS.
Forster's position, in writing, is that Broadmoor's answer "may still be no... as you yourself are not
related to the patient", that she "cannot say this with 100% certainty", that the archive "would still be
willing to put your application forward to the hospital and ask", and that "there is always a possibility
they might agree to release some information." Corrected in MASTER_NOTES.

The barrier that IS close to absolute is the other one, from the first letter: re-use in print, which she
says they are "very unlikely" to grant while the file is closed. So the honest position is that some
summary information might conceivably be released to Rik personally, and that almost none of it could
lawfully appear in the book before 2036.

**2. The descent point remains true and remains Coda material, but it is an argument about lineage, not
about archive policy.** William killed the only people who could ever have inherited the right to ask.
That is arithmetic and it is worth stating. What must NOT be written, now, is that the archive's rules
make the file unreachable in principle, because the archive itself has said it would ask on Rik's behalf.
Keep the two claims apart in the prose.

**3. Rik had already told her he had "a lead on William Gouldstone's family", and she had already answered
the no-living-family argument.** The draft letter written earlier this evening proposed to explain to her
that nobody can meet the descent test. She got there first, fifteen months ago. That paragraph has been
cut; see the revised draft.

## OUTSTANDING, AND CHEAP

Forster offered, in June 2025, a £22 transcription of page three of the coroner's papers, which is
Milne's deposition, because the scan had been smoothed by the software. Page three has since been

### CLAUDE'S ADVICE ON THIS LETTER WAS WRONG, AND IS WITHDRAWN

The draft cut the descent paragraph on the reasoning that Forster had already ruled on the point and that
restating it would "volunteer, in writing, the single fact most likely to sink the application." Rik put
it back. He was right and the advice was wrong, for a reason that was available at the time:

**There was nothing to volunteer.** Forster's own letter of 25 June 2025 already says "you yourself are
not related to the patient". She knew. Withholding it would not have concealed anything, and stating it
plainly changes the frame of the request in his favour: the argument is no longer that Rik fails the
descent test, it is that the test is inapplicable to this particular record, because the class of people
it was written for was destroyed by the patient himself. That invites the hospital to consider an
alternative basis rather than measuring him against a bar he cannot clear. A concealed weakness would
have surfaced at the hospital stage anyway, and worse.

Logged because the reasoning error is a general one and likely to recur: treating a fact as damaging
because it is unfavourable, without first checking whether the other party already holds it.

### THE ADDITION THAT MATTERS: the offer of the manuscript

Rik's own, not in the draft. It is the strongest thing in the letter, because it turns the third question
from a request into an evidenced one: he is not asserting that he is a researcher, he is offering to
show it. Nothing in the manuscript comes from the sealed file, which is itself the point worth making if
she takes him up on it.

**Handling, if she asks:** send the chapters and the outline, and say plainly that none of it draws on
the patient file, that the book is built from the trial papers, the coroner's depositions and the
contemporary press, and that the request is for the file to correct and complete a record already
reconstructed without it. Do not send it unsolicited.

### TWO TYPOS, both minor, one of them Claude's fault

- **"poorbodily"** for "poor bodily". The draft broke that phrase across a line and the space was lost in
  the paste. Claude's formatting, Rik's letter. If a follow-up is sent for any other reason, it can be
  corrected in passing; it is not worth a message of its own, and the meaning is clear.
- **"about 12,000 into the book"** is missing its noun, "words". Same: clear enough in context.

### WHAT TO WATCH FOR IN THE REPLY

1. **The two words on page three**, which would settle "Oswald" and "six/nine" in the transcription.
2. **Whether the case book D/H14/D2/1/1/4 is separately consultable**, and whether his entry carries a
