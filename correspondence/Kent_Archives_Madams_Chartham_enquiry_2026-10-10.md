Subject: Kent County Lunatic Asylum, Chartham: a patient admitted in 1901 (William Madams)

Dear Archives team,

I am researching my family for a work of narrative non-fiction and would be grateful for help with the records of
the Kent County Lunatic Asylum at Chartham (later St Augustine's Hospital), if you hold them.

The patient:
- William Madams (William James Madams), a labourer of Whitstable, born in Whitstable about 1860.
- Admitted to the Kent County Lunatic Asylum, Chartham, in 1901 (4 September 1901, according to the index to the
  Lunacy Commission's admission registers, MH 94/37).
- Died in the asylum on 23 November 1901; cause given on his death certificate as general paralysis of the insane.

My questions:
1. Do you hold the asylum's admission register, case books and reception orders for 1901, and if so, could you
   tell me the references for his entries?
2. Are records of this date open to the public, or would any access conditions apply?
3. Do you offer a paid search and copying service for researchers working remotely, and what would it cost to have
   his admission entry, reception order and case book entry copied?
4. If the records are held elsewhere, could you point me to the right repository?

Any of these would be of great help: in particular who signed the reception order, the address he was admitted
from, and what was recorded of his family.

With thanks and best wishes,

Rik Ferguson
