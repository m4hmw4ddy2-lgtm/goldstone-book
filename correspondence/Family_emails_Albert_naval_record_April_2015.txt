from me to Steve, Rob (my brother), and my mum 2 Apr 2015 - Subject: Let me introduce your Granddad
Well, your great-granddad, Rob.
Albert Goldstone was born in Hüfening, Hohenzollern in Germany on the 19 June 1886, he stood 5'5" tall, fresh-faced with brown hair and hazel eyes. He had tattoos on both arms, on his right forearm were the words "I love H" and on his left an anchor.

Originally a farm labourer, he enlisted in the navy in 1901 at the age of 15 as a "Boy 2nd class" the lowest rank on board a ship, basically a servant. Inside 1 year he was already "Boy 1st class" and two years later was made "Ordinary Seaman" an apprenticeship to become an "Able Seaman" an ambition he realised 3.5 years later in January 1908.
From 1913 to 1918 he held a sub-rating of Seaman Gunner and in 1917 was Acting Gun Layer 2nd Class
He was promoted to Leading Seaman in July 1916 during WWI and eventually made Petty Officer (New Scheme)  in 1918 finally getting his "King's Certificate" (discharge) in 1919 for heart disease.
I'm sure there's a lot more interesting information on the attached Naval Record, including the full list of all the vessels he served on, but I haven't deciphered it all yet!
 
Rik
