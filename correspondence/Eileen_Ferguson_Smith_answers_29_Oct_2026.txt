# EILEEN FERGUSON SMITH (Rik's mother), ANSWERS TO THE BOOK'S QUESTIONS, relayed by Rik, 29 October 2026
# Her message as Rik pasted it, verbatim. Testimony and tradition: notes/PART_SIX_family_memory_log.md (29 Oct, Eileen's answers).
# Permission to use her memories and testimony: given (Rik, 28 Oct 2026).

OK fit what I know into the questions above. 
I met your dad in September 1964. His dad had died in Feb 1962. His grandparents were hardly ever mentioned but it does mean Vera lost her father and husband in the same year which I didn’t know. Is it possible that this strange aunty had moved in with Vera as she had been living with her brother do you think? I didn’t know Vera was in the Civil
service, perhaps her marriage certificate would give evidence and she quite like worked there until March 1943, your dad was born  21st April 1943. When I met her she was working for ( Bob I think). He had a building company on the Luton Rd in Chatham. ( Nanny cared for his mother as a District Nurse). Vera ran the business side, payroll, book keeping etc. of course Aunty Lil
Was a civil servant in London too. She was an Executive Officer in Holborn somewhere.
The conversation about Brian ( more a comment than a conversation) was 1966 sometime. I know it was in their dining room in Dagmar Rd and I said something about church. I think it was likely to have arisen talking about our wedding. I wanted to marry in St Paul’s Church in Chatham as that was where I had gone with aunty Bowsher and it meant a lot to me. When I said that was the reason she was very emotional and said ‘How can you believe in God, my little brother ( and I think now she actually did say ‘my little brother Brian’) died. If there was a God he would have saved him’.
Interesting only Albert was mentioned on more than one occasion between the sisters that there dad had been a local councillor in Luton, Chatham Borough Council. ( Not Medway Council as it is today). As you know the four sisters were very close but their parents, any aunts and uncles ( apart from ‘mad’ aunty who they joked about) were never mentioned. 
Sue was the one in 1987 when she visited us in Sharnbrook, said she had discovered the story about Albert and the Goldstone name. Obviously that was almost the same one as the lie you discovered.
As far as I remember Sue said he was German and was called Goldstein. He had managed to come to England after the war started and changed his name to Goldstone. She thought he was Jewish and I must admit I thought Vera possibly looked as if she might have Jewish heritage! I am not aware that she knew any more than that. And the only link to the navy is what Malcom has written just about the Arethusa. I don’t know where this story came from. It must be what his mother had been told.

Yes! I think George Stock was possibly Vera’s boyfriend. He lived on the Luton Rd too probably almost opposite Bobs building company. It was Bob that took Vera to Switzerland when she brought the Smoking Man home! As I said, Nanny (my mum’s mum) nursed his mother and she told me Bob wanted to marry Vera but she refused.

Just had another thought Mick and his family were members of the RNA (Royal Navy Association) which had there club on the Luton Rd too ( Connaught Rd is off the Luton Rd). We went regularly on a Sunday night before were married, they a ‘proper’ band and Mick’s Dad used to sing sometimes. David Ferguson, Mick’s cousin has a photo of these times. Now knowing Albert’s connection, I bet he went there too. Is it worth following up with the RNA to see if they have a record of his membership? Obviously never met him because he had died two years before I met your Dad!
